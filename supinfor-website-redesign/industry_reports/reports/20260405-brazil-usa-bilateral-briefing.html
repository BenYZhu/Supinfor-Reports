核心判断Core Assessment

本周巴美关系呈现"政治高压、经贸博弈、民间热络"的三层结构。政治层面，美国众议院司法委员会报告直接介入巴西司法主权争议，博索纳罗家族在美活动将巴西内政国际化，卢拉政府以主权话语强硬回应；经贸层面，美国USTR报告将Pix定性为贸易壁垒、关税调整持续冲击巴西出口，但稀土矿产合作开辟了新的利益交汇点；民间层面，世界杯热身赛在美举办、球员跨国流动、文化交流保持活跃。总体而言，巴美关系正处于"竞争性共存"状态——政治摩擦升温但未失控，经贸分歧加深但合作空间犹存。 This week's Brazil-US relations exhibited a three-layered structure of "political high pressure, economic-trade gaming, and vibrant people-to-people exchanges." Politically, the US House Judiciary Committee report directly intervened in Brazil's judicial sovereignty dispute, the Bolsonaro family's US activities internationalized Brazilian domestic politics, and the Lula government responded firmly with sovereignty rhetoric. In trade, the USTR report designated Pix as a trade barrier and tariff adjustments continued to impact Brazilian exports, yet rare earth mineral cooperation opened new convergence points. At the people-to-people level, World Cup warm-up matches in the US, cross-border player movements, and cultural exchanges remained active. Overall, Brazil-US relations are in a state of "competitive coexistence" — political friction is heating up but not out of control, trade divergences are deepening but cooperation space still exists.

The report's political intent is clear — to garner international public opinion support for the Bolsonaro family ahead of the 2026 elections, while providing political ammunition for US tech companies to resist Brazilian digital regulation. The Supreme Court's swift response indicates Brazil views this as a sovereignty red line issue.

Brazil's central bank built a national-level financial infrastructure independent of the US payment system through Pix, directly impacting Visa and Mastercard's fee revenue in the Brazilian market (estimated at hundreds of billions of reais). The US inclusion of Pix in the Section 301 framework means it could be used as leverage in tariff negotiations. However, the Lula government's firm stance indicates Pix has become a symbolic sovereignty issue for Brazil, with minimal room for concession.
