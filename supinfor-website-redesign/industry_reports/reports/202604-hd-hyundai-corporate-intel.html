执行摘要Executive Summary

本周期内，HD Hyundai（现代重工）集团旗下工程机械板块呈现三个核心特征：合并整合效应全面释放、智能化与自动化产品密集发布、全球经销网络加速扩张。HD建设机械（HD현대건설기계与HD현대인프라코어合并后的新实体）交出了合并后首份季报，营业利润同比暴增88.3%，验证了"One Team"协同战略的有效性。与此同时，DEVELON和Hyundai双品牌在欧洲、东南亚、拉美等市场密集推出新品和技术方案，展现出强劲的产品创新节奏。During this period, HD Hyundai's construction equipment division exhibited three core characteristics: full realization of merger synergies, intensive launch of intelligent and automated products, and accelerated global dealer network expansion. HD Construction Equipment (the new entity formed by the merger of HD Hyundai Construction Equipment and HD Hyundai Infracore) delivered its first post-merger quarterly report with operating profit surging 88.3% YoY, validating the effectiveness of the "One Team" synergy strategy. Meanwhile, the dual brands DEVELON and Hyundai intensively launched new products and technology solutions across Europe, Southeast Asia, and Latin America, demonstrating a robust product innovation cadence.
