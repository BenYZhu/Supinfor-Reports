一、矿业装备：自主化与电气化深度融合进入商业化冲刺I. Mining Equipment: Autonomy & Electrification Enter Commercialization Sprint

洞察 1.1Insight 1.1

小松-卡特-利勃海尔三巨头以"AHS+电动+软件"三位一体争夺矿山价值链Komatsu-CAT-Liebherr Compete for Mining Value Chain with "AHS + Electric + Software" Trinity

关键洞察：全球矿业装备竞争已从单一设备销售升级为"自主运输系统（AHS）+电气化动力+数字化管理平台"的生态之争。小松与卡特彼勒合计占据全球自主卡车品牌市场74%份额，但利勃海尔凭借与Fortescue的475台零排放设备大单强势切入，三方正围绕矿山全生命周期价值展开全面竞争。Key Insight: Global mining equipment competition has evolved from single equipment sales to an ecosystem battle of "Autonomous Haulage System (AHS) + Electrified Power + Digital Management Platform." Komatsu and Caterpillar together hold 74% of the global autonomous truck market, but Liebherr has entered strongly with a 475-unit zero-emission equipment deal with Fortescue. All three are competing for full mining lifecycle value.

  • 小松全球部署780台自主卡车，累计运输超100亿吨物料，10台卡车运行时长突破10万小时；与丰田合作开发自主轻型车，推出凿岩机和推土机远程操控系统Komatsu deployed 780 autonomous trucks globally, transported over 10 billion tons, 10 trucks exceeded 100,000 hours; partnered with Toyota on autonomous light vehicles, launched remote control for rock drills and dozers
  • 卡特彼勒MineStar Command系统已在三大洲部署，累计自主行驶超2亿英里、运输物料超91亿吨；新增电池电动技术支持与充电管理功能；完成收购矿业软件公司RPMGlobal，打造软硬件一体化矿场解决方案CAT MineStar Command deployed across 3 continents, over 200M autonomous miles, 9.1B+ tons transported; added battery-electric support and charging management; acquired RPMGlobal for integrated mining solutions
  • 利勃海尔与Fortescue签署475台设备协议（含360台自主电池电动矿卡、55台电动挖掘机、60台电池推土机），联合开发的AHS集成能源管理系统支持6MW快充，预计2026年初完成全自主电池电动方案验证Liebherr signed 475-unit deal with Fortescue (360 autonomous battery-electric haul trucks, 55 electric excavators, 60 battery dozers), AHS with 6MW fast charging, full autonomous battery-electric validation expected early 2026

洞察 1.2Insight 1.2

电气化路径分化：纯电、混动、氨燃料三条技术路线并行推进Electrification Path Divergence: Pure Electric, Hybrid, Ammonia Fuel Advance in Parallel

关键洞察：矿业与工程机械的动力转型并非"一刀切"走向纯电，而是根据应用场景形成纯电（城市/封闭矿区）、混动（过渡方案）、氨燃料（偏远矿区）三条并行路线。小松与AFC Energy合作研发氨燃料发动机，为无法部署充电基础设施的偏远矿区提供第三选择，这一差异化布局值得关注。Key Insight: Mining and construction machinery power transition is not a one-size-fits-all move to pure electric. Three parallel routes have formed based on application scenarios: pure electric (urban/enclosed mines), hybrid (transitional), and ammonia fuel (remote mines). Komatsu's ammonia fuel engine R&D with AFC Energy provides a third option for remote mines without charging infrastructure.

  • 纯电路线：徐工向Fortescue交付783kW电机的XC9260BEWL装载机和XC9260BEWD推土机（4亿美元订单首批）；日立推出198kWh锂电池的ZX135-7EB电动挖掘机Pure electric: XCMG delivered 783kW XC9260BEWL loader and XC9260BEWD dozer to Fortescue ($400M order first batch); Hitachi launched ZX135-7EB electric excavator with 198kWh lithium battery
  • 混动路线：小松与印尼United Tractors推出HB365-1混动挖掘机，省油17%、每小时减排13kg CO2；Lundin Mining将小松930E-4矿卡改装为混动电动设备Hybrid: Komatsu and Indonesia's United Tractors launched HB365-1 hybrid excavator, 17% fuel savings, 13kg CO2 reduction/hour; Lundin Mining converted Komatsu 930E-4 to hybrid-electric
  • 氨燃料路线：小松与AFC Energy签署约200万美元联合开发协议，研发氨裂解技术与工业内燃机整合方案，目标为偏远矿区提供可持续替代燃料Ammonia fuel: Komatsu signed ~$2M joint development agreement with AFC Energy for ammonia cracking technology integration with industrial ICE, targeting sustainable fuel for remote mines
  • 电池供应链：卡特彼勒风投领投塞尔维亚ElevenEs B轮融资，支持建设1GWh LFP电池超级工厂，2027年开始交付Battery supply chain: CAT Ventures led Serbia's ElevenEs Series B, supporting 1GWh LFP battery gigafactory, delivery starting 2027

四、AI与数字化：从概念验证到生产力释放的关键转折IV. AI & Digitalization: Critical Turning Point from PoC to Productivity

洞察 4.1Insight 4.1

AI在矿山场景率先实现规模化生产力提升，数字孪生缩短勘探周期AI Achieves Scaled Productivity Gains in Mining; Digital Twins Shorten Exploration Cycles

Brand protection and product quality control in emerging markets are becoming hidden risks for Chinese companies going global.

核心趋势概括Core Trends

2026年2月的工程机械行业呈现三条清晰的主线：一是矿业装备的自主化与电气化深度融合进入商业化冲刺阶段，小松、卡特彼勒、利勃海尔三巨头以"AHS+电动+软件平台"三位一体模式争夺矿山全生命周期价值；二是中国头部企业（徐工、中联重科）以大额海外订单、本地化服务网络和标准输出为抓手，加速从设备出口商向生态输出者转型；三是AI数据中心基建成为重型设备需求的新增长极，美国设备融资额创20年新高，卡特彼勒和约翰迪尔同步推进"硬件+软件+服务"一体化转型以捕获经常性收入。February 2026 construction machinery industry shows three clear themes: (1) Mining equipment autonomy and electrification entering commercialization sprint, with Komatsu, CAT, and Liebherr competing via "AHS + electric + software" trinity for full mining lifecycle value; (2) Chinese leaders (XCMG, Zoomlion) accelerating transformation from equipment exporters to ecosystem exporters through large overseas orders, localized service networks, and standards output; (3) AI data center infrastructure becoming a new growth engine for heavy equipment demand, with US equipment financing hitting 20-year highs and CAT/Deere pushing "hardware + software + service" integration for recurring revenue.
