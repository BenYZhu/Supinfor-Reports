01

品牌画像综述Brand Profile Overview

阅读提示Reading Note

本报告基于30天的媒体数据，是品牌形象的短周期扫描快照。所有画像判断仅反映本周期内的媒体报道特征，不代表品牌的长期定位或历史积累。建议结合多个周期的持续监测，形成更完整的品牌画像认知。 This report is based on 30 days of media data, serving as a short-cycle snapshot of brand image. All profile judgments only reflect media coverage characteristics within this period and do not represent the brand's long-term positioning or historical accumulation. It is recommended to combine continuous monitoring across multiple cycles for a more complete brand profile understanding.

本周期内，大疆在全球媒体中初步呈现出一个技术统治力与地缘政治压力并存的复杂品牌形象。一方面，品牌以极高的产品发布密度（Lito系列无人机、Osmo Pocket 4、Mic Mini 2、ROMO扫地机器人等）持续强化"消费级影像技术标杆"的定位；另一方面，北京无人机禁售令和美国FCC禁令构成了前所未有的双重监管压力，使品牌在"技术领先者"和"地缘风险承受者"之间形成了显著的形象张力。 During this period, DJI initially presents a complex brand image in global media where technological dominance coexists with geopolitical pressure. On one hand, the brand continuously reinforces its positioning as "the benchmark for consumer-grade imaging technology" through an extremely high product launch density (Lito series drones, Osmo Pocket 4, Mic Mini 2, ROMO robot vacuum, etc.); on the other hand, Beijing's drone sales ban and the US FCC ban constitute unprecedented dual regulatory pressure, creating significant image tension between "technology leader" and "geopolitical risk bearer."
