中国车企集体跻身全球前十，全球汽车竞争格局进入"中国时刻"Chinese Automakers Collectively Enter Global Top 10, Marking the "China Moment" in Global Auto Competition

二、电动化战略分化II. EV Strategy Divergence

洞察 2.1Insight 2.1

Stellantis巨亏223亿欧元后重启燃油车，激进电动化战略遭遇最惨痛的市场教训Stellantis Restarts ICE After €22.3B Loss; Aggressive EV Strategy Meets Harshest Market Lesson

关键洞察：Stellantis从2024年盈利55亿欧元到2025年亏损223亿欧元的断崖式逆转，核心原因是对电动车市场需求的严重误判。这一案例表明，电动化转型的节奏把控比方向选择更为关键——过快的转型速度在消费者尚未准备好的市场中会转化为巨额库存和资产减值。Key Insight: Stellantis' cliff-like reversal from €5.5B profit in 2024 to €22.3B loss in 2025 was primarily caused by severe misjudgment of EV market demand. This case demonstrates that pacing of EV transition is more critical than direction — overly rapid transition in unprepared markets converts into massive inventory and asset impairment.

  • 2025年全年净亏损223亿欧元，2024年盈利55亿欧元，一年内逆转278亿欧元2025 full-year net loss €22.3B, 2024 profit €5.5B, €27.8B reversal within one year
  • 仅2025年下半年亏损201亿欧元，计提254亿欧元特殊支出（电动车项目减值、合同终止成本等）H2 2025 alone lost €20.1B, with €25.4B special charges (EV project impairment, contract termination costs, etc.)
  • 新任CEO Antonio Filosa宣布重启燃油车生产，回归汽油、柴油发动机车型New CEO Antonio Filosa announced restart of ICE production, returning to gasoline and diesel models
  • 北美业务亏损22亿美元，美国UAW工会员工2025年无绩效奖金North America business lost $2.2B; US UAW workers received no performance bonus in 2025
  • 欧洲高管称若无政府补贴，欧洲电动车销量将难以维持European executives stated European EV sales would be unsustainable without government subsidies

全文总结Full Summary

核心趋势概括Core Trends

2026年2月的全球汽车行业呈现出三条清晰的主线：第一，中国车企从"市场参与者"升级为"格局塑造者"，BYD、SAIC、Geely集体进入全球前十，同时通过海外建厂、充电网络布局和品牌渗透实现深度全球化；第二，电动化转型进入"分化期"，Stellantis巨亏后重启燃油车、特斯拉产能利用率跌破四成、PHEV实际排放超标300%，表明行业正在从"全面冲刺电动化"转向"务实评估转型节奏"；第三，竞争维度从"电动化"延伸至"智能化"，现代9万亿韩元押注AI与氢能、XPeng布局机器人与软件授权、Waymo无人出租车市场份额超Lyft，预示着下一阶段的竞争将围绕AI、自动驾驶和多元化业务展开。The global auto industry in Feb 2026 shows three clear themes: First, Chinese automakers upgraded from "market participants" to "landscape shapers" — BYD, SAIC, Geely collectively entered the global top 10 while achieving deep globalization through overseas factories, charging networks, and brand penetration. Second, EV transition entered a "divergence period" — Stellantis restarted ICE after massive losses, Tesla utilization fell below 40%, PHEV actual emissions exceeded standards by 300%, indicating the industry is shifting from "all-out EV sprint" to "pragmatic pace assessment." Third, competition extended from "electrification" to "intelligence" — Hyundai's KRW 9T AI & hydrogen bet, XPeng's robot & software licensing, Waymo's robotaxi share exceeding Lyft signal that next-stage competition will center on AI, autonomous driving, and diversified businesses.
