一、本周总览：日本与中国关联度评估I. Weekly Overview: Japan-China Relevance Assessment

本周中日关系呈现"政冷经冷、安全对抗升级"的严峻态势。靖国神社春祭引发新一轮外交摩擦，日本海自护卫舰首次在高市任期内通过台湾海峡，叠加日本废除战后致命武器出口禁令，三大事件形成共振，将中日安全互信推至近年低点。与此同时，中国对日稀土磁铁出口管制效果初显，日本加速推进供应链"去中国化"，经济脱钩风险持续累积。值得注意的是，日本多个经贸团体正积极筹备访华，释放出在政治寒冬中维系经济纽带的务实信号。总体而言，本周中日关系处于"安全对抗加剧、经济摩擦扩大、民间交流承压"的多重困境之中。This week, China-Japan relations exhibited a severe posture of "political chill, economic chill, and escalating security confrontation." The Yasukuni Shrine (靖国神社) spring festival triggered a new round of diplomatic friction; a JMSDF escort ship transited the Taiwan Strait for the first time under PM Takaichi Sanae's (高市早苗) tenure; and Japan abolished its postwar ban on lethal weapons exports. These three events resonated together, pushing China-Japan security trust to its lowest point in recent years. Meanwhile, China's rare earth magnet export controls on Japan are showing initial effects, and Japan is accelerating supply chain "de-Sinicization," with economic decoupling risks continuing to accumulate. Notably, multiple Japanese business and trade organizations are actively preparing visits to China, signaling a pragmatic desire to maintain economic ties amid the political winter. Overall, China-Japan relations this week are mired in a multi-layered predicament of "intensifying security confrontation, expanding economic friction, and strained people-to-people exchanges."

事件概要：Summary: 中国对日稀土磁铁出口出现显著下滑，2025年3月同比减少27.2%至184吨，9个月来首次跌破200吨大关，出口管制效果初步显现。China's rare earth magnet exports to Japan declined significantly, falling 27.2% year-on-year to 184 tons in March 2025 — dropping below the 200-ton mark for the first time in 9 months, with export control effects beginning to materialize.

洞察Insight

稀土管制是中国对日经济施压的精准工具——既能对日本高端制造业（电动汽车、风力发电、精密电机）形成供应链压力，又可针对性打击涉军企业。27.2%的降幅和跌破200吨的心理关口，标志着管制从"政策信号"进入"实质影响"阶段。未来，日本将加速推进稀土供应链多元化（澳大利亚、加拿大等替代来源），但短期内替代产能难以填补缺口，日本制造业将面临成本上升和供应不确定性的双重压力。Rare earth controls are China's precision tool for economic pressure on Japan — capable of creating supply chain pressure on Japan's high-end manufacturing (EVs, wind power, precision motors) while targeting military-linked enterprises. The 27.2% decline and breach of the psychological 200-ton threshold mark the transition from "policy signal" to "substantive impact." Going forward, Japan will accelerate rare earth supply chain diversification (Australia, Canada, and other alternative sources), but replacement capacity cannot fill the gap in the short term, leaving Japanese manufacturers facing dual pressures of rising costs and supply uncertainty.
