一、执行摘要I. Executive Summary

本周美国营商环境围绕三条主线展开：税改法案进入报税季检验期，平均退税增长11%至3462美元，但油价飙升和民众"获得感"不足使共和党政治红利大打折扣；关税退款系统（CAPE）4月20日上线，涉及约1270亿美元退款，进口商迎来现金流修复窗口，但消费者难以直接受益；美伊冲突持续冲击供应链与能源成本，霍尔木兹海峡航运中断推高油价超40%，化肥、航油、物流成本全线上涨，中小企业和低收入家庭首当其冲。对外商投资者而言，需重点关注美国对中国车企的市场准入封锁信号、纽约州针对超富群体的新税提案，以及反垄断执法力度的持续升级。

This week's U.S. business climate revolved around three main themes: The tax reform bill enters its first filing season test — average refunds rose 11% to $3,462, but soaring oil prices and a lack of public "felt benefit" have undercut the GOP's political dividend; The CAPE tariff refund system goes live on April 20, covering roughly $127 billion in refunds and offering importers a cash-flow recovery window, though consumers are unlikely to benefit directly; The U.S.-Iran conflict continues to hammer supply chains and energy costs — the Strait of Hormuz shipping disruption has pushed oil prices up over 40%, with fertilizer, jet fuel, and logistics costs rising across the board, hitting SMEs and low-income households hardest. Foreign investors should pay close attention to the U.S. market-access blockade signals against Chinese automakers, New York State's new tax proposals targeting the ultra-wealthy, and the continued escalation of antitrust enforcement.

三、重点事件解读III. Key Events Analysis

事件一 约15条

最高法院裁定关税违法，1270亿美元退款系统4月20日上线Supreme Court Rules Tariffs Unlawful; $127B Refund System Launches April 20

事件概述Overview

美国最高法院此前裁定特朗普政府依据《国际紧急经济权力法》（IEEPA）征收的关税违法，政府需向进口商退还已缴关税。美国海关与边境保护局（CBP）开发的CAPE退款系统将于4月20日上线，首批处理约1270亿美元退款，涉及超5.6万家进口商。退款需企业主动申请，审核通过后60-90天到账。

The U.S. Supreme Court previously ruled that tariffs imposed by the Trump administration under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) were unlawful, requiring the government to refund collected duties to importers. The CAPE refund system developed by U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) will go live on April 20, initially processing approximately $127 billion in refunds involving over 56,000 importers. Refunds require proactive application and take 60–90 days after approval.

涉及方Parties Involved

CBP、国际贸易法院、超3000家起诉企业、进口商群体

CBP, Court of International Trade, 3,000+ plaintiff companies, importer community

潜在影响Potential Impact

这是美国贸易政策史上罕见的大规模关税退还事件。对进口商而言，退款将显著改善现金流，部分企业已通过出售退款债权给对冲基金提前套现。但CNBC调查显示，无一家受访企业计划将退款传递给消费者，退款红利大概率留存在企业端。同时，特朗普政府正通过301条款调查等替代路径恢复关税壁垒，财长贝森特表示关税或于7月初恢复至此前水平，企业面临"退款-再征"的政策循环风险。

This is a rare large-scale tariff refund event in U.S. trade policy history. For importers, the refunds will significantly improve cash flow — some companies have already cashed out early by selling refund claims to hedge funds. However, a CNBC survey found that none of the companies interviewed plan to pass refunds on to consumers, meaning the windfall will likely stay at the corporate level. Meanwhile, the Trump administration is pursuing alternative paths to restore tariff barriers via Section 301 investigations, and Treasury Secretary Bessent indicated tariffs could return to previous levels by early July, leaving businesses facing a "refund-then-reimpose" policy cycle risk.

事件二 约8条

美国商务部长明确拒绝中国车企进入美国市场U.S. Commerce Secretary Explicitly Rejects Chinese Automakers' U.S. Market Entry

事件概述Overview

美国商务部长卢特尼克在华盛顿对话会上直接拒绝比亚迪在美设立合资工厂的请求，明确表示不会允许任何中国电动车企业进入美国汽车业。福特CEO法利也公开呼吁阻止中国车企入美，称其获政府巨额补贴拥有不公平优势，并存在数据收集的国家安全风险。当前美国对中国产电动车征收100%关税。

U.S. Commerce Secretary Lutnick directly rejected BYD's request to establish a joint venture factory in the U.S. at a Washington dialogue, explicitly stating that no Chinese EV company would be allowed into the American auto industry. Ford CEO Farley also publicly called for blocking Chinese automakers, citing massive government subsidies creating unfair advantages and national security risks from data collection. The U.S. currently imposes 100% tariffs on Chinese-made EVs.

涉及方Parties Involved

美国商务部、比亚迪、福特、通用汽车、美国贸易代表办公室

U.S. Department of Commerce, BYD, Ford, General Motors, USTR

潜在影响Potential Impact

这是美国政府迄今对中国车企最明确的市场封锁信号。对中国车企而言，通过合资方式进入美国市场的路径已被堵死，短期内只能继续深耕墨西哥、加拿大等周边市场。同时，特朗普政府正考虑调整《美墨加协定》汽车贸易规则，提高进口车本土零部件占比要求，这将进一步压缩中国车企通过第三国迂回进入美国的空间。对在美投资的外国车企（如大众、通用韩国工厂），关税成本仍是持续压力。

This is the clearest market-blockade signal the U.S. government has sent to Chinese automakers to date. For Chinese EV companies, the joint-venture path into the U.S. market is now closed, leaving them to focus on Mexico, Canada, and other peripheral markets in the near term. Meanwhile, the Trump administration is considering adjustments to USMCA auto trade rules to raise domestic parts content requirements, further squeezing Chinese automakers' ability to enter the U.S. via third countries. For foreign automakers already invested in the U.S. (e.g., Volkswagen, GM Korea plants), tariff costs remain a persistent pressure.

事件三 约30条

纽约州推出500万美元以上第二住宅附加税New York State Introduces Surcharge Tax on Second Homes Over $5 Million

事件概述Overview

纽约州州长霍楚尔与纽约市长曼达尼联合推出针对纽约市价值500万美元以上第二住宅的年度附加税（pied-à-terre tax），预计影响约1.3万套房产，年创收至少5亿美元，用于填补纽约市54亿美元预算缺口。特朗普在社交平台公开抨击该政策"将摧毁纽约"，对冲基金大亨丹尼尔·洛布、比尔·阿克曼等也强烈反对。

New York Governor Hochul and NYC Mayor Mandani jointly introduced an annual surcharge tax (pied-à-terre tax) on second homes in New York City valued over $5 million, expected to affect approximately 13,000 properties and generate at least $500 million annually to help close the city's $5.4 billion budget gap. Trump publicly attacked the policy on social media as "destroying New York," and hedge fund moguls Daniel Loeb and Bill Ackman also strongly opposed it.

涉及方Parties Involved

纽约州政府、纽约市政府、Citadel CEO肯·格里芬、特朗普、房地产行业

New York State Government, NYC Government, Citadel CEO Ken Griffin, Trump, real estate industry

潜在影响Potential Impact

该税直接影响在纽约持有高端房产的外国投资者和跨国企业高管。格里芬此前已将Citadel总部从纽约迁至迈阿密，该税可能加速高净值人群和资本向佛罗里达、德克萨斯等低税州转移。对计划在纽约设立办公室或购置房产的外商企业，需将此税纳入成本考量。不过，巴黎经济学院研究显示富人因税迁出的实际比例很低，纽约市场韧性较强。

This tax directly affects foreign investors and multinational executives holding high-end properties in New York. Griffin had already relocated Citadel's headquarters from New York to Miami, and this tax may accelerate the migration of high-net-worth individuals and capital to low-tax states like Florida and Texas. Foreign companies planning to set up offices or purchase property in New York should factor this tax into their cost calculations. However, research from the Paris School of Economics shows that the actual rate of wealthy individuals relocating due to taxes is very low, and the New York market remains resilient.

事件四 约15条

Live Nation被裁定非法垄断，反垄断执法力度升级Live Nation Ruled an Illegal Monopoly; Antitrust Enforcement Escalates

事件概述Overview

纽约联邦陪审团裁定Ticketmaster母公司Live Nation在大型演唱会场馆市场存在有害垄断行为，在22个州每张票多收1.72美元。此前Live Nation已与司法部达成2.8亿美元和解，但33个州继续推进诉讼。法官将确定后续处罚措施，可能要求Live Nation出售部分场馆。

A New York federal jury ruled that Ticketmaster parent company Live Nation engaged in harmful monopolistic practices in the major concert venue market, overcharging $1.72 per ticket across 22 states. Live Nation had previously reached a $280 million settlement with the DOJ, but 33 states continue to pursue litigation. The judge will determine further penalties, potentially requiring Live Nation to divest some venues.

涉及方Parties Involved

Live Nation/Ticketmaster、美国司法部、33个州检察长

Live Nation/Ticketmaster, U.S. DOJ, 33 state attorneys general

潜在影响Potential Impact

这是近年来美国反垄断执法的标志性案例，释放出监管机构对市场垄断行为零容忍的信号。同期，加州司法部对亚马逊的价格操纵诉讼也在推进，FTC对StubHub处以1000万美元罚款。对在美运营的外商企业，需高度关注美国反垄断执法趋势，尤其是在票务、电商、科技等领域的市场支配地位审查。

This is a landmark antitrust enforcement case in recent years, signaling zero tolerance from regulators toward monopolistic behavior. Concurrently, the California DOJ's price manipulation lawsuit against Amazon is advancing, and the FTC fined StubHub $10 million. Foreign companies operating in the U.S. should closely monitor antitrust enforcement trends, especially market dominance reviews in ticketing, e-commerce, and technology sectors.

事件五 约20条

美伊冲突持续冲击能源供应链，油价飙升超40%U.S.-Iran Conflict Continues to Disrupt Energy Supply Chains; Oil Prices Surge Over 40%

事件概述Overview

美伊冲突导致霍尔木兹海峡航运几乎完全中断，全球石油供应链遭受严重冲击。美国汽油均价突破每加仑4美元（部分地区达5美元），为2022年8月以来首次。油价上涨传导至化肥、航油、物流等全产业链，消费者物价涨幅扩大。美国石油行业警告特朗普不要在和平协议中允许伊朗收取海峡通行费。

The U.S.-Iran conflict has nearly completely disrupted shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, severely impacting the global oil supply chain. Average U.S. gasoline prices have surpassed $4 per gallon (reaching $5 in some areas) for the first time since August 2022. Oil price increases have cascaded through the entire supply chain — fertilizer, jet fuel, logistics — widening consumer price inflation. The U.S. oil industry has warned Trump against allowing Iran to collect strait transit fees in any peace agreement.

涉及方Parties Involved

美国石油协会、雪佛龙、波斯湾产油国、美国消费者

American Petroleum Institute, Chevron, Persian Gulf oil producers, U.S. consumers

潜在影响Potential Impact

能源成本飙升是本周营商环境最大的系统性风险。7-Eleven北美计划关闭645家门店、小企业进口成本上涨5%-8%、美国家庭今年汽油支出将额外增加740美元——这些都是油价冲击的直接后果。雪佛龙加大委内瑞拉原油进口、美国经巴拿马运河石油出口接近四年高位，反映出供应链正在寻找替代路径。对依赖海运的外商企业，物流成本和交货周期的不确定性将持续数月。

Soaring energy costs represent the biggest systemic risk to the business climate this week. 7-Eleven North America plans to close 645 stores, small business import costs have risen 5%–8%, and U.S. households will spend an additional $740 on gasoline this year — all direct consequences of the oil price shock. Chevron is increasing Venezuelan crude imports, and U.S. oil exports via the Panama Canal are near a four-year high, reflecting supply chains seeking alternative routes. For foreign companies reliant on maritime shipping, logistics cost and delivery timeline uncertainty will persist for months.

事件六 约60条

税改法案报税季检验——退税增长但民众"获得感"不足Tax Reform Filing Season Test — Refunds Up but Public "Felt Benefit" Lacking

事件概述Overview

2025年7月通过的《One Big Beautiful Bill Act》（后更名《Working Families Tax Cuts》）进入首个完整报税季。数据显示：平均退税3462美元，同比增长11%；超5300万纳税人使用了新增抵扣项（小费免税、加班费抵扣、老年人额外扣除等）。但皮尤民调显示超六成美国人仍认为缴税过高，仅27%认为税改帮助了自己。

The "One Big Beautiful Bill Act" (later renamed "Working Families Tax Cuts"), passed in July 2025, has entered its first full filing season. Data shows average refunds of $3,462, up 11% year-over-year; over 53 million taxpayers used new deduction items (tip tax exemption, overtime pay deduction, senior additional deduction, etc.). However, a Pew poll shows over 60% of Americans still feel they pay too much in taxes, and only 27% believe the reform helped them.

涉及方Parties Involved

IRS、美国财政部、共和党、民主党、纳税人群体

IRS, U.S. Treasury, Republican Party, Democratic Party, taxpayer community

潜在影响Potential Impact

税改对企业端的影响更为直接——100%即时设备折旧抵扣永久化、SALT抵扣上限提至4万美元、pass-through实体QBI抵扣延长。这些政策降低了在美运营企业的实际税负。但IRS因预算削减裁员27%，税务执法能力下降，可能导致合规环境变化。对外商企业，建议关注SALT抵扣上限调整对高税州运营成本的影响。

The tax reform's impact on the corporate side is more direct — permanent 100% immediate equipment depreciation, SALT deduction cap raised to $40,000, and extended QBI deduction for pass-through entities. These policies reduce the effective tax burden for businesses operating in the U.S. However, IRS staffing has been cut 27% due to budget reductions, weakening enforcement capacity and potentially altering the compliance environment. Foreign companies should monitor how the SALT deduction cap adjustment affects operating costs in high-tax states.

四、趋势研判IV. Trend Assessment

1

美国正构建多层次的对华汽车产业防线The U.S. Is Building a Multi-Layered Defense Against China's Auto Industry

本周多条资讯交叉印证：商务部长直接拒绝比亚迪合资请求、福特CEO公开呼吁封锁中国车企、政府考虑调整《美墨加协定》汽车规则提高本土零部件占比、100%关税壁垒持续。这不是单一事件，而是系统性的产业保护战略——从关税壁垒、市场准入、贸易规则到舆论造势，形成了完整的封锁链条。

Multiple reports this week cross-confirm: the Commerce Secretary directly rejected BYD's JV request, Ford's CEO publicly called for blocking Chinese automakers, the government is considering USMCA auto rule adjustments to raise domestic parts content, and the 100% tariff wall persists. This is not an isolated event but a systematic industrial protection strategy — from tariff barriers and market access to trade rules and narrative shaping, forming a complete blockade chain.

研判Assessment

中国车企短期内进入美国市场的可能性极低。但中国低成本高科技车在墨西哥（比亚迪占电动车销量七成）和加拿大（年进口4.9万辆）的快速扩张，正在从侧翼施压美国市场。外商车企在美投资需评估本土化生产的长期成本优势，同时关注美墨加贸易规则调整对跨境供应链的影响。

The likelihood of Chinese automakers entering the U.S. market in the near term is extremely low. However, the rapid expansion of Chinese low-cost, high-tech vehicles in Mexico (BYD holds 70% of EV sales) and Canada (49,000 units imported annually) is applying flanking pressure on the U.S. market. Foreign automakers investing in the U.S. should evaluate the long-term cost advantages of localized production while monitoring USMCA trade rule adjustments' impact on cross-border supply chains.

2

关税政策进入"退款-再征"循环期，企业需做长期规划Tariff Policy Enters a "Refund-Then-Reimpose" Cycle; Businesses Need Long-Term Planning

普华永道调查显示86%的美国高管已将关税作为长期规划前提。最高法院裁定IEEPA关税违法后，1270亿美元退款即将启动，但特朗普政府正通过301条款调查恢复关税，财长称7月初可能恢复此前税率。15%全球关税将于7月24日到期。超半数美国企业因关税利润受压，70%延迟大额投资。

A PwC survey shows 86% of U.S. executives now treat tariffs as a long-term planning assumption. After the Supreme Court ruled IEEPA tariffs unlawful, $127 billion in refunds are about to begin, but the Trump administration is restoring tariffs via Section 301 investigations, with the Treasury Secretary indicating rates could return to previous levels by early July.
