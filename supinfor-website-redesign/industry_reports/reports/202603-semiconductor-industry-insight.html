全文总结Full Summary

核心趋势概括Core Trends

2026年3月的全球半导体行业呈现出"AI驱动的供需失衡"这一核心主线：上游HBM4三巨头同步量产争夺英伟达Rubin平台订单，中游台积电先进制程产能成为最大瓶颈（3nm严重短缺、1.4nm路线图确立两年领先），下游AI芯片需求推动全行业涨价潮（德州仪器最高涨85%）。全球半导体产能正从亚洲集中走向多极分散——印度美光工厂投产、韩国1500万亿韩元投资计划、欧洲Chips Act 2.0、越南升级制造能力。中国半导体出口额前两月同比增长72.6%，正从"中转站"向"高价值供应商"转型。The global semiconductor industry in March 2026 centers on "AI-driven supply-demand imbalance": upstream HBM4 triple giants compete for NVIDIA Rubin platform orders; midstream TSMC advanced node capacity is the biggest bottleneck (severe 3nm shortage, 1.4nm roadmap establishing two-year lead); downstream AI chip demand drives industry-wide price hikes (TI up to 85%). Global semiconductor capacity is diversifying from Asia-centric to multi-polar — India Micron factory launch, Korea's ₩1,500T investment plan, Europe's Chips Act 2.0, Vietnam manufacturing upgrade. China semiconductor exports grew 72.6% YoY in first two months, shifting from "transit hub" to "high-value supplier."
