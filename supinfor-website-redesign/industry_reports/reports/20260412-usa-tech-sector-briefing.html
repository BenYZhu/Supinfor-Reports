2.1 阿尔忒弥斯II任务：人类重返月球的里程碑Artemis II Mission: A Milestone in Humanity's Return to the Moon

事件脉络Event Timeline

4月1日，NASA太空发射系统（SLS）火箭搭载猎户座飞船"Integrity"号从肯尼迪航天中心发射，搭载指挥官Reid Wiseman、飞行员Victor Glover、任务专家Christina Koch及加拿大宇航员Jeremy Hansen四人。任务历时10天，完成绕月飞行，最远距地球252,756英里，打破阿波罗13号保持56年的纪录。4月10日晚，飞船以约25,000英里时速重返大气层，在圣迭戈附近太平洋成功溅落。

On April 1, NASA's Space Launch System (SLS) rocket carrying the Orion spacecraft "Integrity" launched from Kennedy Space Center with Commander Reid Wiseman, Pilot Victor Glover, Mission Specialist Christina Koch, and Canadian astronaut Jeremy Hansen. The 10-day mission completed a lunar flyby, reaching a maximum distance of 252,756 miles from Earth, breaking the 56-year record held by Apollo 13. On the evening of April 10, the spacecraft re-entered the atmosphere at approximately 25,000 mph and successfully splashed down in the Pacific Ocean near San Diego.

技术验证与风险Technical Validation & Risks

本次任务最大技术悬念在于猎户座飞船隔热罩。2022年阿尔忒弥斯I无人试飞时，Avcoat隔热材料因透气性不足出现超100处脱落。NASA未更换隔热罩，而是调整再入轨迹，缩短极端高温暴露时间。前NASA工程师Charles Camarda公开质疑该方案如同"俄罗斯轮盘"，但最终飞船安全溅落。此外，任务期间还出现太空马桶故障和服务舱推进系统阀门泄漏，后者需大规模重新设计才能用于后续任务。

The biggest technical concern of this mission was the Orion spacecraft's heat shield. During the unmanned Artemis I test flight in 2022, the Avcoat heat shield material experienced over 100 instances of charring and loss due to insufficient breathability. NASA chose not to replace the heat shield but instead adjusted the re-entry trajectory to reduce extreme heat exposure time. Former NASA engineer Charles Camarda publicly criticized the approach as "Russian roulette," but the spacecraft ultimately splashed down safely. Additionally, a space toilet malfunction and a service module propulsion system valve leak occurred during the mission; the latter requires major redesign before use in subsequent missions.

后续计划与挑战Future Plans & Challenges

NASA计划2027年执行阿尔忒弥斯III（近地轨道对接测试），2028年阿尔忒弥斯IV实现月球南极着陆。SpaceX星舰和蓝色起源Blue Moon正竞争登月舱合同。然而，白宫最新预算提案拟削减NASA科学预算近50%、总预算减少23%，波音SLS火箭单次发射成本约40亿美元远超预算，独立分析师普遍认为2028年登月目标不现实。同时，中国登月方案更简洁，美中太空竞赛压力持续。

NASA plans to execute Artemis III (near-Earth orbit docking test) in 2027 and achieve a lunar south pole landing with Artemis IV in 2028. SpaceX's Starship and Blue Origin's Blue Moon are competing for the lunar lander contract. However, the White House's latest budget proposal seeks to cut NASA's science budget by nearly 50% and total budget by 23%. Boeing's SLS rocket costs approximately $4 billion per launch, far exceeding budget. Independent analysts widely consider the 2028 lunar landing target unrealistic. Meanwhile, China's lunar landing approach is more streamlined, maintaining pressure in the U.S.-China space race.

影响评估Impact Assessment

阿尔忒弥斯计划带动了广泛的产业链参与——仅密歇根州就有78家实体参与供应。该州正筹建太空创新枢纽，全球太空经济预计2035年达1.8万亿美元。但NASA面临人才流失、预算削减的现实困境，商业航天公司将在后续任务中扮演更重要角色。

The Artemis program has driven broad supply chain participation — Michigan alone has 78 entities involved. The state is building a space innovation hub, and the global space economy is projected to reach $1.8 trillion by 2035. However, NASA faces real challenges of talent attrition and budget cuts, and commercial space companies will play an increasingly important role in subsequent missions.

2.2 Anthropic Mythos模型：AI网络安全的"分水岭时刻"Anthropic Mythos Model: A "Watershed Moment" for AI Cybersecurity

事件脉络Event Overview

Anthropic本周发布Claude Mythos Preview模型，该通用AI模型可自主发现并利用主流操作系统和浏览器的零日漏洞，在测试中找出数千个此前未被发现的严重系统漏洞（部分存在长达27年）。因能力过强，Anthropic史上首次限制产品发布，仅通过"Project Glasswing"项目向亚马逊、苹果、谷歌、英伟达等约50家关键基础设施企业限量开放。

Anthropic released the Claude Mythos Preview model this week. This general-purpose AI model can autonomously discover and exploit zero-day vulnerabilities in mainstream operating systems and browsers, identifying thousands of previously unknown critical system vulnerabilities during testing (some existing for up to 27 years). Due to its extraordinary capabilities, Anthropic restricted a product release for the first time in its history, offering limited access only to approximately 50 critical infrastructure companies including Amazon, Apple, Google, and NVIDIA through "Project Glasswing."

政府紧急响应Government Emergency Response

美国财长Scott Bessent与美联储主席Jerome Powell在华盛顿财政部召集花旗、摩根士丹利等系统性重要银行高管闭门会议，警示Mythos及同类AI产品带来的网络安全风险。副总统JD Vance也参与了与科技企业的协调。IMF总裁格奥尔基耶娃公开表示全球暂无能力保护国际货币体系免受此类AI威胁。加拿大央行同步召集金融监管机构讨论应对。

U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Fed Chair Jerome Powell convened a closed-door meeting at the Treasury Department in Washington with executives from systemically important banks including Citi and Morgan Stanley, warning of cybersecurity risks posed by Mythos and similar AI products. Vice President JD Vance also participated in coordination with tech companies. IMF Managing Director Georgieva publicly stated that the world currently lacks the capacity to protect the international monetary system from such AI threats. The Bank of Canada simultaneously convened financial regulators to discuss responses.

市场冲击Market Impact

Mythos发布引发软件股大幅抛售，标普500软件与服务指数周跌1.6%，年内跌幅扩至26%，高盛软件股篮子单周跌5%。Palantir因Michael Burry看空叠加Mythos竞争担忧，本周跌约13%。与此同时，Anthropic企业付费客户占比快速攀升至约30%，逼近OpenAI的35%，Claude下载量同期增三倍达2,100万。

The Mythos release triggered a major sell-off in software stocks. The S&P 500 Software & Services Index fell 1.6% for the week, extending its year-to-date decline to 26%. Goldman Sachs' software stock basket dropped 5% in a single week. Palantir fell approximately 13% this week due to Michael Burry's short position combined with Mythos competition concerns. Meanwhile, Anthropic's enterprise paying customer share rapidly climbed to approximately 30%, closing in on OpenAI's 35%, while Claude downloads tripled to 21 million during the same period.

深层影响Deeper Implications

《华盛顿邮报》评论指出，Mythos意味着具备一定编码能力的普通人即可借此攻击美国大量数字基础设施。安全业界对威胁程度分歧巨大——支持者认为其实现了多阶段漏洞利用链开发能力，质疑者则认为只是现有AI能力的升级。但无论如何，该事件已推动AI网络安全从行业议题升级为国家安全议题。

The Washington Post commented that Mythos means ordinary people with basic coding skills could use it to attack vast swaths of America's digital infrastructure. The security community is deeply divided on the threat level — supporters believe it has achieved multi-stage exploit chain development capability, while skeptics view it as merely an upgrade of existing AI capabilities. Regardless, the event has elevated AI cybersecurity from an industry issue to a national security issue.

2.3 AI产业竞争：三强争霸与监管围堵AI Industry Competition: Three-Way Battle & Regulatory Siege

竞争格局Competitive Landscape

本周AI产业呈现三条主线并行。其一，Meta推出Muse Spark模型（Meta Superintelligence Labs首款产品），在部分基准测试中优于OpenAI和Google Gemini顶级产品，Meta AI美区日活达峰值，iOS下载量增87%，跃居应用商店免费榜第6。其二，Anthropic凭借Claude Code（年化收入超25亿美元）和Mythos持续抢占市场，估值达3,800亿美元。其三，OpenAI面临多线压力——马斯克变更诉讼策略要求撤销其营利化转型、佛州总检察长启动国安调查、版权侵权诉讼中专家证人表现不佳被法官批评。

Three main threads ran in parallel in the AI industry this week. First, Meta launched the Muse Spark model (the first product from Meta Superintelligence Labs), outperforming top products from OpenAI and Google Gemini in some benchmarks. Meta AI's U.S. daily active users hit a peak, iOS downloads surged 87%, and it jumped to #6 on the App Store free chart. Second, Anthropic continued to capture market share with Claude Code (annualized revenue exceeding $2.5B) and Mythos, reaching a $380B valuation. Third, OpenAI faced pressure on multiple fronts — Musk changed his litigation strategy to demand reversal of its for-profit conversion, Florida's AG launched a national security investigation, and an expert witness performed poorly in a copyright infringement lawsuit, drawing criticism from the judge.

企业AI投资加速Enterprise AI Investment Accelerates

亚马逊CEO贾西在股东信中披露AWS AI年化营收150亿美元，自研芯片业务年化营收超200亿美元，2026年计划投入约2,000亿美元资本支出。亚马逊还将密西西比州数据中心投资从130亿提升至250亿美元，并暗示未来可能对外销售自研AI芯片，直接挑战英伟达。CoreWeave与Anthropic达成新合作，与Meta签订210亿美元AI算力协议，与OpenAI合同总价值提升至约224亿美元。

Amazon CEO Jassy disclosed in his shareholder letter that AWS AI annualized revenue reached $15B, in-house chip business annualized revenue exceeded $20B, and the company plans approximately $200B in capital expenditure for 2026. Amazon also increased its Mississippi data center investment from $13B to $25B and hinted at potentially selling its proprietary AI chips externally, directly challenging NVIDIA. CoreWeave reached a new partnership with Anthropic, signed a $21B AI compute agreement with Meta, and increased its total contract value with OpenAI to approximately $22.4B.

监管围堵Regulatory Siege

佛州总检察长以国安风险和ChatGPT关联校园枪击案为由调查OpenAI；xAI起诉科罗拉多州AI反歧视法案，称其违反第一修正案；联邦层面《取下法案》实现首例AI深度伪造儿童色情定罪；美众议院外交事务委员会拟审议至少10项芯片出口管制法案。AI企业同时面临创新竞赛和合规压力的双重夹击。

Florida's AG investigated OpenAI citing national security risks and ChatGPT's connection to a school shooting. xAI sued over Colorado's AI anti-discrimination law, claiming it violates the First Amendment. At the federal level, the TAKE IT DOWN Act secured the first conviction for AI deepfake child exploitation. The House Foreign Affairs Committee plans to review at least 10 chip export control bills. AI companies face the dual squeeze of innovation competition and compliance pressure.
