C.3 关键风险提示Key Risk Alerts

风险 1：美国关税与贸易保护主义升级Risk 1: US Tariffs & Trade Protectionism Escalation

美国对马来西亚商品加征关税的风险是当前最大外部威胁。马来西亚对美贸易顺差约 260 亿美元，已引起美方关注。IMF 预计此因素将使 2026 年 GDP 增速从 4.6% 降至 4.3%。对企业的影响：以马来西亚为基地出口美国的企业可能面临成本上升；部分中国企业通过马来西亚"转口"规避关税的策略可能受到审查。The risk of US tariffs on Malaysian goods is the biggest external threat. Malaysia's ~USD 26 billion trade surplus with the US has drawn attention. IMF estimates this could reduce 2026 GDP growth from 4.6% to 4.3%. Business impact: companies exporting to the US from Malaysia may face rising costs; some Chinese firms' "re-export" strategies via Malaysia may face scrutiny.

应对建议Mitigation

关注美国贸易政策动态，评估供应链布局的合规性，考虑产品原产地规则的影响。Monitor US trade policy developments; assess supply chain compliance; consider rules of origin implications.

严重度：中高Severity: Medium-High

风险 2：中美科技脱钩对 E&E 供应链的冲击Risk 2: US-China Tech Decoupling Impact on E&E Supply Chain

马来西亚 E&E 产业深度嵌入中美两国供应链，中美科技脱钩可能迫使企业在两个体系间做出选择。美国对华半导体出口管制可能波及在马来西亚运营的中国关联企业。Malaysia's E&E industry is deeply embedded in both US and China supply chains; tech decoupling may force companies to choose between the two systems. US semiconductor export controls on China may affect China-linked enterprises operating in Malaysia.

应对建议Mitigation

密切跟踪美国实体清单和出口管制政策变化，确保供应链合规，考虑建立"中国+1"和"美国友岸"双轨供应链布局。Closely track US Entity List and export control policy changes; ensure supply chain compliance; consider establishing dual-track "China+1" and "US friend-shoring" supply chain layouts.

严重度：中Severity: Medium

风险 3：林吉特汇率波动Risk 3: MYR Exchange Rate Volatility

林吉特受美元走势、全球风险偏好和大宗商品价格多重影响，2025-2026 年在 4.2-4.4 区间波动。央行降息可能进一步施压汇率。对企业的影响：以林吉特计价的收入在换汇时可能缩水，进口成本波动影响利润率。MYR is influenced by USD trends, global risk appetite, and commodity prices, fluctuating in the 4.2–4.4 range in 2025–2026. Central bank rate cuts may further pressure the exchange rate. Business impact: MYR-denominated revenues may shrink upon conversion; import cost volatility affects margins.

应对建议Mitigation

建立系统性汇率对冲机制，考虑使用远期合约和期权工具，在合同中加入汇率调整条款。Establish systematic FX hedging mechanisms; consider forward contracts and options; include exchange rate adjustment clauses in contracts.

严重度：中Severity: Medium

风险 4：税收政策调整频繁Risk 4: Frequent Tax Policy Changes

马来西亚近年税收政策变化较多（服务税扩围至 8%、全球最低税率 15% 实施、2026 年预算调整），企业税务合规成本上升。Malaysia has seen frequent tax policy changes in recent years (service tax expanded to 8%, global minimum tax 15% implemented, 2026 budget adjustments); corporate tax compliance costs are rising.

应对建议Mitigation

聘请本地专业税务顾问，及时跟踪财政预算和税法修订，充分利用 NIIF 和双边税收协定的优惠条款。Engage local professional tax advisors; promptly track budget and tax law amendments; fully leverage NIIF and bilateral DTA preferential provisions.

严重度：中低Severity: Medium-Low

风险 5：高端人才竞争加剧Risk 5: Intensifying High-End Talent Competition

AI、半导体设计、数据科学等领域人才缺口明显，新加坡的虹吸效应导致部分高端人才外流。Significant talent gaps in AI, semiconductor design, data science; Singapore's brain drain effect causes some high-end talent outflow.

应对建议Mitigation

提前布局人才储备，与本地大学建立合作，提供有竞争力的薪酬和职业发展路径，考虑从中国外派核心技术人员。Plan talent reserves early; establish university partnerships; offer competitive compensation and career paths; consider seconding core technical staff from China.
