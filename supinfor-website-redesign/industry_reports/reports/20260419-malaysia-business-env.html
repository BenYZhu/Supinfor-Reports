一、执行摘要1. Executive Summary

本周马来西亚营商环境被一场由中东冲突引发的能源危机深度重塑。马来西亚日产原油35万桶、日耗70万桶，38%进口原油经霍尔木兹海峡运输，4月燃油补贴飙升至70亿令吉（冲突前仅7亿令吉），INSAP警告通胀率或升至6.1%-6.6%，为2008年以来最高。与此同时，投资端呈现韧性——霹雳州2025年投资额达157.6亿令吉创历史新高（同比+80%），清真产业出口685.2亿令吉创纪录（同比+10.9%），马股IPO市场活跃。但财政压力急剧上升，GST重启讨论加速，比亚迪设厂争议暴露外资政策协调短板，Q1 GDP初估5.3%略低于预期，均构成中期营商环境的关键变量。

This week, Malaysia's business climate was profoundly reshaped by an energy crisis triggered by the Middle East conflict. Malaysia produces 350,000 barrels of crude oil daily but consumes 700,000 barrels, with 38% of imported crude transiting the Strait of Hormuz. April fuel subsidies surged to RM 7 billion (pre-conflict: only RM 0.7 billion), and INSAP warned inflation could reach 6.1%-6.6%, the highest since 2008. Meanwhile, the investment side showed resilience — Perak's 2025 approved investment hit RM 15.76 billion, an all-time high (YoY +80%), halal exports reached a record RM 68.52 billion (YoY +10.9%), and Bursa Malaysia's IPO market remained active. However, fiscal pressure is mounting sharply, GST reinstatement discussions are accelerating, the BYD factory dispute exposed coordination gaps in foreign investment policy, and Q1 GDP at 5.3% came in slightly below expectations — all constituting key variables for the medium-term business environment.

能源危机与财政补贴压力——马来西亚面临"史上最严重石油供应中断" Energy Crisis & Fiscal Subsidy Pressure — Malaysia Faces "Worst Oil Supply Disruption in History"

事件概述Event Overview

中东冲突导致霍尔木兹海峡航运受阻，马来西亚日产原油35万桶但日耗70万桶，38%进口原油经该海峡运输。柴油零售价7周内暴涨124.7%，4月燃油补贴飙升至约70亿令吉（其中RON95补贴从3亿升至40亿，柴油补贴从4亿升至30亿），是冲突前（约7亿令吉）的10倍。Petronas派遣Ocean Thunder号从伊拉克运回100万桶原油以稳定供应，确认国内燃油供应可维持至2026年6月底。政府同步上调BUDI柴油援助至每月400令吉、稻农耕作补贴至每公顷300令吉，4月补贴总额含7,500万令吉柴油专项援助。

The Middle East conflict disrupted shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Malaysia produces 350,000 barrels of crude daily but consumes 700,000, with 38% of imported crude transiting the strait. Diesel retail prices surged 124.7% in 7 weeks. April fuel subsidies soared to approximately RM 7 billion (RON95 subsidies rose from RM 0.3B to RM 4B, diesel subsidies from RM 0.4B to RM 3B), 10 times the pre-conflict level (approximately RM 0.7B). Petronas dispatched the Ocean Thunder to transport 1 million barrels of crude from Iraq, confirming domestic fuel supply can be maintained until end of June 2026. The government simultaneously raised BUDI diesel assistance to RM 400/month, paddy farming subsidies to RM 300/hectare, with April subsidies including RM 75 million in dedicated diesel assistance.

涉及方Parties Involved

财政部、Petronas、国家经济行动理事会、INSAP Ministry of Finance, Petronas, National Economic Action Council, INSAP

潜在影响Potential Impact

按当前油价（超100美元/桶），全年补贴或达720亿令吉，远超2026年预算基准（65-70美元/桶），财政赤字可能突破GDP的4%。4个月已耗尽全年84亿令吉补贴预算，补贴成本增收覆盖比仅约27%。对在马运营企业而言，柴油涨价直接推高物流、建筑、农业等领域运营成本，面条粿条等食品已涨价5%-10%，供应链成本传导效应将在Q3-Q4全面显现。

At current oil prices (above USD 100/barrel), annual subsidies could reach RM 72 billion, far exceeding the 2026 budget baseline (USD 65-70/barrel), with the fiscal deficit potentially breaching 4% of GDP. Four months have already exhausted the full-year RM 8.4 billion subsidy budget, with subsidy cost-to-revenue coverage at only about 27%. For businesses operating in Malaysia, diesel price hikes directly increase operating costs in logistics, construction, and agriculture. Noodle and rice noodle prices have already risen 5%-10%, and supply chain cost pass-through effects will fully materialize in Q3-Q4.

霹雳州投资创历史新高——RM157.6亿，外资暴涨超1,700倍 Perak Investment Hits All-Time High — RM 15.76B, FDI Surges Over 1,700x

事件概述Event Overview

霹雳州2025年获批准投资总额达157.6亿令吉，较2024年增长约80%，创历史新高。制造业为核心引擎，外资涨幅超1,700倍，跻身全国制造业投资前五。州政府推动北中南三大工业布局：北部吉辇绿色工业园发展电子电气产业，中部银谷科技园（Silver Valley Tech Park）预计吸资140亿令吉，南部丹绒马林汽车高科技谷携手吉利打造新能源车枢纽。QL Foods与宝腾分别投建清真食品与电动车工厂。州财政收入近14.94亿令吉，连续4年盈余。

Perak's 2025 approved investment totaled RM 15.76 billion, up approximately 80% from 2024, an all-time high. Manufacturing was the core engine, with FDI surging over 1,700 times, placing Perak among the nation's top five for manufacturing investment. The state government is driving a north-central-south industrial layout: the northern Kerian Green Industrial Park for E&E, the central Silver Valley Tech Park expected to attract RM 14 billion, and the southern Tanjung Malim Automotive Hi-Tech Valley partnering with Geely to build an NEV hub. QL Foods and Proton are building halal food and EV factories respectively. State revenue reached nearly RM 1.494 billion, with surpluses for 4 consecutive years.

涉及方Parties Involved

霹雳州政府、吉利汽车、QL Foods、宝腾、MIDA Perak State Government, Geely Auto, QL Foods, Proton, MIDA

潜在影响Potential Impact

霹雳州的投资爆发为外商提供了新的区域选择，尤其是新能源汽车、电子电气和清真食品三大赛道。但比亚迪设厂争议（见事件三）提示，联邦与州政府在外资政策协调上仍存在摩擦，投资者需关注政策执行的一致性。吉打州同期吸引278亿令吉投资（制造业全国第一），砂拉越未来5年拟吸引1,680亿令吉投资，各州竞争态势加剧。

Perak's investment surge offers foreign investors new regional options, especially in NEV, E&E, and halal food. However, the BYD factory dispute (see Event 3) highlights ongoing friction between federal and state governments on FDI policy coordination — investors should monitor policy execution consistency. Kedah attracted RM 27.8 billion in the same period (top nationally for manufacturing), and Sarawak aims to attract RM 168 billion over 5 years, intensifying inter-state competition.

比亚迪设厂争议——外资政策协调短板暴露 BYD Factory Dispute — FDI Policy Coordination Gap Exposed

事件概述Event Overview

比亚迪在霹雳州丹绒马林的新能源车组装厂因贸工部（MITI）新增条款而停工。贸工部要求80%产品外销、20%内销，初期40%零部件本地化率，整装进口车最低售价25万令吉以上、本地组装车15万令吉以上。霹雳州6名议员联合质疑条款过于严苛，州政府指责贸工部政策仓促、缺乏沟通。贸工部副部长沈志勤回应称政策旨在保障国内生态与产业链发展，比亚迪4年在马售3万辆车已获3.6亿令吉奖掖。首相安华表示将与贸工部讨论相关条件。

BYD's NEV assembly plant in Tanjung Malim, Perak was halted due to new conditions imposed by MITI. MITI required 80% export / 20% domestic sales, initial 40% local content ratio, minimum pricing of RM 250,000 for CBU imports and RM 150,000 for CKD vehicles. Six Perak assemblymen jointly questioned the overly stringent conditions, with the state government accusing MITI of hasty, poorly communicated policy. MITI Deputy Minister Sim Chee Keong responded that the policy aims to protect the domestic ecosystem and supply chain, noting BYD has sold 30,000 vehicles in Malaysia over 4 years and received RM 360 million in incentives. PM Anwar indicated he would discuss the conditions with MITI.

涉及方Parties Involved

贸工部（MITI）、霹雳州政府、比亚迪、首相署 MITI, Perak State Government, BYD, Prime Minister's Office

潜在影响Potential Impact

此事件是本周对外商投资信心冲击最大的单一事件。联邦与州政府在外资条件上的公开分歧，以及"临门变阵"式的政策调整，向国际投资者释放了政策不确定性信号。马华前领导人及多名议员警告，若处理不当将损害国家投资信誉。对计划在马设厂的制造业企业，尤其是新能源汽车领域，需密切关注后续政策走向。

This was the single most impactful event for foreign investment confidence this week. The open disagreement between federal and state governments on FDI conditions, along with "last-minute" policy changes, sent policy uncertainty signals to international investors. Former MCA leaders and multiple assemblymen warned that mishandling could damage national investment credibility. Manufacturing companies planning to set up in Malaysia, especially in the NEV sector, should closely monitor subsequent policy developments.

GST重启讨论加速——INSAP提出12个月公众咨询路线图 GST Reinstatement Accelerates — INSAP Proposes 12-Month Public Consultation Roadmap

事件概述Event Overview

INSAP总监温京才提出系统性财税改革方案：建议先合理化销售与服务税（SST）结构，再启动至少12个月的恢复5% GST公众咨询，预计GST年收约770亿令吉，可产生结构性盈余。同时建议向B40家庭每月发放200令吉、M40家庭150令吉的12个月危机转移援助，覆盖492万户家庭，年耗108亿令吉。此外建议90天内启动战略石油储备可行性研究，公布完整自动定价机制公式。专家指出若布伦特原油均价持续在105-120美元，2026年预算或需调整，财政赤字可能超GDP的4%。

INSAP Director Wan Jingcai proposed a systematic fiscal reform plan: first rationalize the Sales and Services Tax (SST) structure, then launch at least 12 months of public consultation on reinstating 5% GST, with projected annual GST revenue of approximately RM 77 billion, generating a structural surplus. He also recommended RM 200/month for B40 households and RM 150/month for M40 households as 12-month crisis transfer assistance, covering 4.92 million households at an annual cost of RM 10.8 billion. Additionally, he proposed launching a strategic petroleum reserve feasibility study within 90 days and publishing the complete automatic pricing mechanism formula. Experts noted that if Brent crude averages USD 105-120, the 2026 budget may need adjustment, with the fiscal deficit potentially exceeding 4% of GDP.

涉及方Parties Involved

INSAP、财政部、国家经济行动理事会 INSAP, Ministry of Finance, National Economic Action Council

潜在影响Potential Impact

GST重启将从根本上改变马来西亚的税收结构，对所有在马运营企业产生直接影响。770亿令吉的年收入预期意味着企业需重新评估定价策略和合规成本。12个月咨询期为企业提供了政策准备窗口，但也意味着税制不确定性将持续至2027年。SST结构调整可能先行，企业应提前评估影响。

GST reinstatement would fundamentally change Malaysia's tax structure, directly impacting all businesses operating in the country. The projected RM 77 billion annual revenue means companies need to reassess pricing strategies and compliance costs. The 12-month consultation period provides a policy preparation window but also means tax uncertainty will persist until 2027. SST structural adjustments may come first — businesses should evaluate impacts proactively.

马来西亚-澳大利亚能源安全合作——双边贸易RM786.3亿 Malaysia-Australia Energy Security Cooperation — Bilateral Trade RM 78.63B

事件概述Event Overview

澳大利亚总理阿尔巴尼斯4月15-17日访马，与安华举行四眼会谈，聚焦能源安全合作。双方发布能源供应保障联合声明，签署清真红肉合作谅解备忘录。澳大利亚供应马来西亚96%进口液化天然气，马来西亚向澳供应约17%燃油进口，2025年双边贸易额达786.3亿令吉。安华表示将优先保障国内燃油需求，剩余供应才交给澳方。安华还透露可让Petronas与俄罗斯谈判石油供应，保障能源储备。

Australian PM Albanese visited Malaysia April 15-17, holding a four-eyes meeting with Anwar focused on energy security cooperation. Both sides issued a joint statement on energy supply assurance and signed an MOU on halal red meat cooperation. Australia supplies 96% of Malaysia's imported LNG, while Malaysia supplies about 17% of Australia's fuel imports. 2025 bilateral trade reached RM 78.63 billion. Anwar stated domestic fuel needs would be prioritized, with remaining supply allocated to Australia. Anwar also revealed that Petronas could negotiate oil supply with Russia to secure energy reserves.

涉及方Parties Involved

马来西亚首相署、澳大利亚总理府、Petronas、外交部 Malaysia PM's Office, Australian PM's Office, Petronas, Ministry of Foreign Affairs

潜在影响Potential Impact

马澳能源互依关系在危机中进一步深化，为两国企业在LNG、可再生能源、清洁技术领域创造合作机遇。马来西亚同时寻求俄罗斯石油供应渠道，显示其在能源外交上采取多元化策略。对在马能源相关企业，供应链多元化和能源安全将成为中期战略重点。

The Malaysia-Australia energy interdependence deepened further during the crisis, creating cooperation opportunities for companies in LNG, renewable energy, and clean technology. Malaysia's simultaneous pursuit of Russian oil supply channels demonstrates a diversified energy diplomacy strategy. For energy-related businesses in Malaysia, supply chain diversification and energy security will become medium-term strategic priorities.

INSAP通胀预警——6.1%-6.6%，492万户家庭受冲击 INSAP Inflation Warning — 6.1%-6.6%, 4.92 Million Households Impacted

事件概述Event Overview

INSAP预测马来西亚通胀率将达6.1%-6.6%，为2008年以来最高。生活成本全面影响将在2026年Q3-Q4显现，492万户家庭受冲击。B40低收入家庭每月实际收入损失约165令吉（占中位数收入4.8%），M40中等收入家庭损失242-355令吉。柴油涨价已波及供应链，即便原油价格缓和，供应端成本上涨仍将持续（"第二波通胀"）。国行则维持较温和预期，预计2026年通胀率1.5%-2.5%，认为BUDI95补贴、能源净出口国地位及令吉走强可缓解冲击。

INSAP forecasts Malaysia's inflation rate will reach 6.1%-6.6%, the highest since 2008. The full cost-of-living impact will materialize in Q3-Q4 2026, affecting 4.92 million households. B40 low-income households face monthly real income losses of approximately RM 165 (4.8% of median income), while M40 middle-income households lose RM 242-355. Diesel price hikes have already rippled through supply chains, and even if crude prices moderate, supply-side cost increases will persist ("second-wave inflation"). Bank Negara maintains a more moderate forecast of 1.5%-2.5% for 2026, citing BUDI95 subsidies, net energy exporter status, and ringgit strengthening as mitigating factors.

涉及方Parties Involved

INSAP、国家银行（BNM）、财政部 INSAP, Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM), Ministry of Finance
