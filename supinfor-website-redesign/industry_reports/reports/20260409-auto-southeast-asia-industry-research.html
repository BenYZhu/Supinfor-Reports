价值链利润分布Value Chain Profit Distribution

东南亚汽车行业的利润分布呈现"微笑曲线"特征：整车制造环节利润率最薄（3–5%），主要受制于激烈的价格竞争和高固定成本；售后市场利润率最高（15–25%），包括维修保养、零配件销售和延保服务；汽车金融利润率同样可观。与全球市场相比，东南亚的本地化特征在于：上游零部件供应链相对薄弱，大量核心零部件依赖进口（尤其是动力电池），这压缩了中游制造商的利润空间。The profit distribution in the SEA automotive industry follows a "smile curve" pattern: vehicle assembly has the thinnest margins (3–5%), constrained by intense price competition and high fixed costs; the aftermarket has the highest margins (15–25%), including maintenance, parts sales, and extended warranties; auto finance margins are also substantial. Compared to global markets, SEA's localization challenge is a relatively weak upstream parts supply chain, with heavy reliance on imports for core components (especially power batteries), which compresses midstream manufacturers' margins.

成长期Growth Era 1990s–2000s

区域分工形成，泰国和印尼成为出口制造中心Regional division of labor formed; Thailand and Indonesia became export manufacturing hubs

泰国成为全球皮卡生产中心，印尼成为 MPV 生产中心Thailand became a global pickup production hub; Indonesia became an MPV production hub

成熟期Maturity Era 2010s

年销量突破 300 万辆，日系品牌占据绝对主导地位Annual sales exceeded 3 million units; Japanese brands held absolute dominance

日系品牌合计份额超 80%，市场格局高度稳定Japanese brands held over 80% combined share; market structure was highly stable

转型期Transition Era 2022–2023

中国 EV 品牌大举进入，电动化浪潮开启Chinese EV brands entered en masse; the electrification wave began

BYD、长城、哪吒等进入泰国市场，泰国成为东南亚 EV 桥头堡BYD, GWM, Neta and others entered the Thai market; Thailand became the EV beachhead in SEA

当前阶段Current Phase 2024–2025

EV 渗透率快速提升，传统市场承压，竞争格局重塑EV penetration rising rapidly; traditional market under pressure; competitive landscape reshaping

新加坡和越南 EV 渗透率达 40%，泰国达 21%；印尼和泰国整体销量下滑；中国品牌份额快速上升Singapore and Vietnam EV penetration reached 40%, Thailand 21%; Indonesia and Thailand overall sales declined; Chinese brand share rising rapidly

竞争格局变化趋势Competitive Landscape Trends

东南亚汽车市场正经历数十年来最大的格局变动。日系品牌份额从 80%+ 下降至 70–75%，且下降趋势仍在加速。中国品牌（以 BYD 为首）凭借 EV 产品快速抢占市场，BYD 2025 年出口东南亚增长 216.8%。本地品牌在各自国家保持稳固地位（Perodua 在马来西亚、VinFast 在越南）。新进入者持续涌入，市场从高度集中向多元化竞争演变。The SEA automotive market is undergoing its most significant structural shift in decades. Japanese brand share has declined from 80%+ to 70–75%, and the downward trend is accelerating. Chinese brands (led by BYD) are rapidly capturing market share through EV products — BYD's SEA exports grew 216.8% in 2025. Local brands maintain solid positions in their home markets (Perodua in Malaysia, VinFast in Vietnam). New entrants continue to flood in, and the market is evolving from high concentration to diversified competition.

C.1 核心结论C.1 Key Conclusions

东南亚汽车行业正处于历史性转折点：传统燃油车市场增长放缓甚至下滑（2025 年整体 -2.3%），但电动化转型带来巨大结构性机会（EV 市场 CAGR 7.2%）。中国品牌正在重塑竞争格局，日系品牌数十年的统治地位首次面临实质性挑战。最大的机会在于 EV 整车及其配套生态（充电、售后、金融），最大的风险在于传统市场持续萎缩和补贴退坡后的需求可持续性。The SEA automotive industry is at a historic turning point: traditional ICE market growth is slowing or declining (overall -2.3% in 2025), but the electrification transition brings massive structural opportunities (EV market CAGR 7.2%). Chinese brands are reshaping the competitive landscape, and Japanese brands' decades-long dominance faces substantive challenges for the first time. The biggest opportunities lie in EV vehicles and their supporting ecosystem (charging, after-sales, finance); the biggest risks are continued traditional market contraction and demand sustainability after subsidy phase-out.

C.4 风险提示C.4 Risk Alerts

1
传统市场销量下滑风险Traditional Market Sales Decline Risk
严重度：高Severity: High

泰国（-15.6%）和印尼（-8.7%）两大传统市场在 2025 年出现显著下滑，反映出消费信心不足、家庭债务高企和 EV 替代效应的叠加影响。若宏观经济未能改善，传统燃油车市场可能进入持续萎缩通道，影响区域整车制造产能利用率和供应链健康度。Thailand (-15.6%) and Indonesia (-8.7%), the two largest traditional markets, saw significant declines in 2025, reflecting weak consumer confidence, high household debt, and EV substitution effects. If macroeconomic conditions don't improve, the traditional ICE market may enter a sustained contraction, affecting regional vehicle manufacturing capacity utilization and supply chain health.

应对思路：Mitigation: 关注 EV 和混动车型的结构性增长机会，避免过度依赖传统燃油车业务。Focus on structural growth opportunities in EV and hybrid vehicles; avoid over-reliance on traditional ICE business.

2
EV 补贴退坡后需求可持续性风险Demand Sustainability Risk After EV Subsidy Phase-out
严重度：中高Severity: Med-High

泰国 EV 3.0/3.5 等补贴政策有明确的时间窗口，补贴金额逐步递减。当前 EV 销量中有相当比例受补贴驱动，补贴退坡后消费者是否愿意以全价购买 EV 存在不确定性。触发条件：补贴到期且电池成本未能同步下降。Thailand's EV 3.0/3.5 subsidies have defined time windows with gradually decreasing amounts. A significant portion of current EV sales are subsidy-driven; uncertainty remains about consumer willingness to purchase EVs at full price after phase-out. Trigger: subsidy expiry without corresponding battery cost reduction.

应对思路：Mitigation: 关注电池成本下降曲线，评估无补贴情景下 EV 与 ICE 的总拥有成本（TCO）对比。Monitor battery cost reduction curves; assess EV vs. ICE total cost of ownership (TCO) comparison in a no-subsidy scenario.

3
中国品牌过度竞争导致价格战Chinese Brand Overcrowding Leading to Price Wars
严重度：中Severity: Medium

BYD、奇瑞、长城、长安、吉利等多家中国品牌同时进入东南亚市场，产品定位和价格区间高度重叠。价格战已在泰国 EV 市场显现，部分品牌以低于成本价销售以抢占份额。长期来看，价格战将压缩全行业利润率，可能导致部分品牌退出。Multiple Chinese brands (BYD, Chery, GWM, Changan, Geely) entering SEA simultaneously with highly overlapping positioning and price ranges. Price wars have already emerged in Thailand's EV market, with some brands selling below cost to capture share. Long-term, price wars will compress industry-wide margins and may force some brands to exit.

应对思路：Mitigation: 差异化竞争策略，避免纯粹的价格竞争。Differentiation strategy; avoid pure price competition.

4
充电基础设施不足制约 EV 普及Insufficient Charging Infrastructure Constraining EV Adoption
严重度：中Severity: Medium

除新加坡外，东南亚各国充电桩密度远低于中国和欧洲。城市地区充电设施逐步完善，但二三线城市和农村地区严重不足。这限制了 EV 从一线城市向更广泛市场的渗透。Except Singapore, charging station density across SEA is far below China and Europe. Urban areas are gradually improving, but tier-2/3 cities and rural areas remain severely underserved. This limits EV penetration beyond tier-1 cities.

应对思路：Mitigation: 关注各国充电基础设施建设规划和投资进展。Monitor charging infrastructure development plans and investment progress across countries.

5
地缘政治风险影响供应链Geopolitical Risk Impacting Supply Chains
严重度：中Severity: Medium

中美贸易摩擦可能波及东南亚汽车供应链。若美国对中国制造的汽车零部件加征关税，可能影响在东南亚组装但使用中国零部件的车辆出口。同时，部分东南亚国家可能在中美之间面临选边压力。US-China trade friction may impact SEA auto supply chains. If the US imposes additional tariffs on Chinese-made auto parts, it could affect vehicles assembled in SEA using Chinese components for export. Some SEA countries may also face pressure to choose sides between the US and China.

应对思路：Mitigation: 供应链多元化，关注各国贸易政策动向。Diversify supply chains; monitor trade policy developments across countries.
