洞察 4.1Insight 4.1

欧盟《产业加速法》与美国去中国化政策形成双重夹击，电池供应链本地化进入强制阶段EU Industrial Acceleration Act & US De-China Policies Create Double Squeeze; Battery Supply Chain Localization Enters Mandatory Phase

关键洞察：欧盟《产业加速法》要求电池核心组件（电芯、正极材料、电解液、隔膜）至少3种为欧盟本土生产，叠加美国禁止新型武器系统使用中国产电池（2028年起）、IRA补贴要求非中国成分等政策，全球电池供应链的区域化重构已从政策倡导进入强制执行阶段。Key Insight: The EU Industrial Acceleration Act requires at least 3 of 4 core battery components (cells, cathode, electrolyte, separator) to be EU-produced. Combined with US bans on Chinese batteries in new weapons systems (from 2028) and IRA non-China content requirements, global battery supply chain regionalization has moved from policy advocacy to mandatory enforcement.

  • 超快充技术跟进：Ultra-Fast Charging: 建议竞争企业在2026年Q3前完成5C级快充电池的产品验证和产线适配，同步推进配套充电基础设施合作Competitors should complete 5C fast-charging battery product validation and line adaptation by Q3 2026, simultaneously advancing charging infrastructure partnerships
  • 海外合规体系建设：Overseas Compliance: 建议在欧洲有业务布局的中国电池企业在2026年内完成至少3个核心环节的本地化供应商认证或自建产能规划Chinese battery companies with European operations should complete localized supplier certification or self-built capacity planning for at least 3 core components in 2026
  • 技术路线对冲：Tech Route Hedging: 建议企业采取"主攻一条、储备一条"的策略，对替代路线保持不低于总研发投入15%的持续跟踪投入Companies should adopt a "focus on one, reserve one" strategy, maintaining at least 15% of total R&D spending on alternative route tracking
