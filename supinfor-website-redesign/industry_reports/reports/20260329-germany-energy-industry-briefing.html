伊朗战争引发能源价格危机：德国经济面临"雪上加霜"Iran War Triggers Energy Price Crisis: Germany's Economy Under Mounting Pressure

事件脉络

2月底美以对伊朗发动军事打击后，伊朗革命卫队封锁霍尔木兹海峡，全球约五分之一的石油和液化天然气运输受阻。布伦特原油价格重回每桶104美元以上，德国柴油价格一度逼近2022年3月的历史峰值（差距仅3.3欧分/升），E10汽油较危机前上涨近30欧分/升。

各方立场与博弈

  • 联邦政府：推出"燃油价格套餐"，核心为加油站每日仅可在12点上调一次油价（参考奥地利模式），违规罚款最高10万欧元，同时强化联邦卡特尔局权限。经济部长赖希表态支持临时上调通勤补贴。
  • 联邦州：多州认为措施力度不足，要求设置燃油价格上限、征收石油企业超额利润税、下调能源税。萨克森-安哈尔特州长批评联邦卡特尔局"若继续按现有方式工作则无存在必要"。
  • 社民党：提议效仿卢森堡设置燃油价格上限，通过对能源巨头征收暴利税补贴通勤者。
  • 经济学家：基尔世界经济研究所所长舒拉里克反对燃油价格上限，认为应让市场传导信号；DIW能源专家肯弗特则指出德国正处于"根本性化石能源危机"。
  • 民众：ZDF民调显示80%民众认为政府措施不足，73%支持临时下调燃油税，58%认为高油价是重大个人负担。

影响评估

  • 联邦经济部长赖希警告，若冲突持续，德国或在4月底至5月出现燃油短缺，高能源成本或致德国经济两年损失约400亿欧元。
  • 德国4月消费者信心指数降至-28.0（两年最低），60%消费者预期能源价格将长期高企。
  • 农业领域柴油和化肥成本暴涨，食品终端价格面临上涨压力。
  • 捷克边境加油站因德国人跨境加油已出现燃油售罄。

后续走向研判

联合政府的燃油价格专项工作组将在4月10日前完成七项新减负措施的审核，包括超额利润税、提高通勤补贴、降低能源税或机动车税、试点燃油折扣、重启备用燃煤电厂等。若伊朗冲突持续超过夏季，德国经济增速或降至0.2%，通胀可能短期升至6%。

Event Timeline

Following the US-Israeli military strikes on Iran in late February, Iran's Revolutionary Guard blockaded the Strait of Hormuz, disrupting roughly one-fifth of global oil and LNG shipments. Brent crude surged back above $104/barrel. German diesel prices briefly approached the March 2022 historic peak (gap of only 3.3 euro cents/liter), while E10 gasoline rose nearly 30 euro cents/liter from pre-crisis levels.

Stakeholder Positions

  • Federal Government: Introduced a "fuel price package" — gas stations may only raise prices once daily at noon (modeled on Austria), with fines up to €100,000 for violations. The Federal Cartel Office's powers were also strengthened. Economy Minister Reiche expressed support for temporarily raising commuter allowances.
  • Federal States: Multiple states consider the measures insufficient, demanding fuel price caps, windfall profit taxes on oil companies, and energy tax reductions. Saxony-Anhalt's Minister-President criticized the Federal Cartel Office as "unnecessary if it continues working as before."
  • SPD: Proposed fuel price caps following Luxembourg's model, funded by windfall taxes on energy giants to subsidize commuters.
  • Economists: Kiel Institute President Schularick opposes fuel price caps, arguing markets should transmit price signals. DIW energy expert Kemfert noted Germany is in a "fundamental fossil energy crisis."
  • Public: ZDF polls show 80% of citizens consider government measures insufficient, 73% support temporary fuel tax cuts, and 58% view high fuel prices as a significant personal burden.

Impact Assessment

  • Economy Minister Reiche warned that if the conflict persists, Germany could face fuel shortages by late April to May. High energy costs could cost the German economy approximately €40 billion over two years.
  • Germany's April consumer confidence index fell to -28.0 (two-year low), with 60% of consumers expecting energy prices to remain elevated long-term.
  • Soaring diesel and fertilizer costs in agriculture are putting upward pressure on food prices.
  • Czech border gas stations have already sold out due to Germans crossing the border to refuel.

Outlook

The coalition's fuel price task force will complete review of seven new relief measures by April 10, including windfall profit taxes, increased commuter allowances, energy or vehicle tax reductions, pilot fuel discounts, and restarting reserve coal-fired power plants. If the Iran conflict extends beyond summer, Germany's GDP growth could drop to 0.2%, with inflation potentially spiking to 6% short-term.

煤电退出之争：默茨质疑2038时间表，能源政策路线分裂Coal Phase-Out Dispute: Merz Questions 2038 Timeline, Energy Policy Divided

事件脉络

3月28日，总理默茨在F.A.Z.2026大会上公开表示，面对能源价格上涨和供电短缺风险，可能需要延长运行中燃煤电厂的使用时间，不再坚持"不切实际的退出计划"。他同时呼吁加快建设燃气电厂，长期寄望核聚变技术，明确反对重启核电。

各方立场

  • 默茨（基民盟）：不愿"仅因过往决议就动摇能源供应核心"，强调需保障德国工业核心竞争力。
  • 绿党：联邦议院绿党能源政策发言人凯尔纳批评此举"既不环保也成本高昂"，指出德国煤电发电量已降至1914年以来最低，退出已由市场驱动。勃兰登堡绿党主席批评默茨"破坏国家行动可靠性"。
  • 能源集团Leag：按约定2038年前关闭所有燃煤电厂，可获约17.5亿欧元补偿，绿党称若政策被破坏则补偿不再合理。

影响评估

这一表态标志着德国能源政策的重大不确定性信号。煤电退出时间表的动摇将影响能源企业的投资规划、碳市场预期以及德国在欧盟气候政策中的信誉。同时，默茨对核聚变的支持（联邦研究部计划2029年前投入24亿欧元）和对小型模块化核反应堆研究的开放态度，预示着德国能源结构讨论将进入新阶段。

Event Timeline

On March 28, Chancellor Merz publicly stated at the F.A.Z. 2026 conference that, facing rising energy prices and power supply risks, coal-fired power plants may need to operate longer, abandoning "unrealistic phase-out plans." He also called for accelerating gas-fired power plant construction, expressed long-term hopes for nuclear fusion, and explicitly opposed restarting nuclear power.

Stakeholder Positions

  • Merz (CDU): Unwilling to "undermine the core of energy supply merely because of past resolutions," emphasizing the need to safeguard Germany's industrial competitiveness.
  • Greens: Bundestag Green energy spokesperson Kellner criticized the move as "neither environmentally friendly nor cost-effective," noting German coal power generation has fallen to its lowest since 1914, with the phase-out already market-driven. Brandenburg's Green party chair accused Merz of "undermining the reliability of state action."
  • Energy group Leag: Under existing agreements, Leag would receive approximately €1.75 billion in compensation for closing all coal plants by 2038. The Greens argue compensation is no longer justified if the policy is undermined.

Impact Assessment

This statement signals major uncertainty in German energy policy. Wavering on the coal phase-out timeline will affect energy companies' investment planning, carbon market expectations, and Germany's credibility in EU climate policy. Meanwhile, Merz's support for nuclear fusion (the Federal Research Ministry plans to invest €2.4 billion by 2029) and openness to small modular reactor research suggest Germany's energy mix debate is entering a new phase.

可再生能源法改革争议：90个县区、450亿欧元项目面临停滞Renewable Energy Law Reform Controversy: 90 Counties, €45B in Projects at Risk

事件脉络

联邦经济部长赖希拟通过两项新法案修改能源转型规则：取消户用太阳能固定上网电价，要求新光伏电站自行售电；推出"电网接入包"，其中核心的Redispatch-Vorbehalt将使电网容量受限地区的可再生能源扩建失去吸引力。

影响范围

据enervis评估，德国约90个县区将受影响，当地在建前的风电光伏项目总值达450亿欧元，面临扩张停滞风险。石勒苏益格-荷尔斯泰因州能源部长戈德施密特直斥该计划为"扩张刹车"，认为其不符合当前化石能源危机下的能源安全需求。

Green Planet Energy认为应加快电网数字化、利用过剩绿电生产绿氢或供热，而非将电网问题归咎于可再生能源。

Event Timeline

Federal Economy Minister Reiche plans to amend energy transition rules through two new bills: eliminating fixed feed-in tariffs for residential solar and requiring new PV installations to sell electricity independently; and introducing a "grid access package," whose core Redispatch-Vorbehalt provision would make renewable energy expansion unattractive in grid-constrained areas.

Scope of Impact

According to enervis analysis, approximately 90 German counties would be affected, with pre-construction wind and solar projects worth €45 billion facing expansion stagnation risks. Schleswig-Holstein's Energy Minister Goldschmidt called the plan an "expansion brake," arguing it contradicts energy security needs amid the current fossil fuel crisis.

Green Planet Energy argues for accelerating grid digitalization and using surplus green electricity to produce green hydrogen or district heating, rather than blaming renewable energy for grid problems.

欧盟数据显示德国燃油涨幅已从初期领先邻国回落至中游水平，波兰则采取了更激进的降税和限价措施。

ADAC data shows fuel prices slightly retreated before Easter, with diesel dropping 7.6 euro cents from Monday's peak, but still far above pre-crisis levels.
The Federal Cartel Office president stated no evidence of oil companies abusing market dominance has been found, but German diesel wholesale prices have shown decoupling from crude oil prices.
EU data shows Germany's fuel price increases have fallen from leading neighboring countries to mid-range levels. Poland has adopted more aggressive tax cuts and price cap measures.

Smart Meters: The Federal Network Agency initiated penalty proceedings against 77 grid operators that failed to meet the 20% installation quota. Germany's smart meter penetration is only 5.5%, far behind most European countries at over 80%.
Cross-Border Heating: The German-Polish "United Heat" project between Görlitz and Zgorzelec broke ground, with total investment of approximately €200 million, aiming for fully renewable heating by 2030.

State Energy Ministers' Conference: Most ministers called for reducing electricity taxes for all users, criticized the Federal Economy Minister's energy policy as "outdated," and demanded expanded renewable energy investment.
