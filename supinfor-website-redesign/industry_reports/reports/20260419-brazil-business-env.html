执行摘要Executive Summary

本周核心发现Key Findings This Week

本周巴西营商环境呈现"外贸强劲、财政承压、地缘扰动"三重特征。一季度巴西对华出口创历史新高达239亿美元，石油出口翻倍增长，中国作为巴西战略能源伙伴的地位进一步巩固；与此同时，政府提交的2027年财政框架法案虽设定GDP 0.5%的盈余目标，但扣除项高达657亿雷亚尔，实际盈余仅约80亿雷亚尔，财政可持续性隐忧加深。中东冲突持续推高能源与物流成本，IPCA通胀预期连续五周上调至4.71%突破目标上限，降息周期面临中断风险。积极面来看，Ibovespa创历史新高、外资年内净流入678亿雷亚尔、EU-Mercosur自贸协定5月1日生效，均为在巴企业带来结构性机遇。

This week, Brazil's business environment exhibited a triple pattern of "robust foreign trade, fiscal pressure, and geopolitical disruption." In Q1, Brazil's exports to China hit a historic high of US$23.9 billion, with oil exports doubling, further consolidating China's position as Brazil's strategic energy partner. Meanwhile, the government's 2027 fiscal framework bill sets a primary surplus target of 0.5% of GDP, but with deductions totaling BRL 65.7 billion, the actual surplus is only about BRL 8 billion, deepening fiscal sustainability concerns. The Middle East conflict continues to push up energy and logistics costs, with IPCA inflation expectations rising for the fifth consecutive week to 4.71%, breaching the target ceiling, and the rate-cutting cycle facing interruption risk. On the positive side, the Ibovespa hit an all-time high, foreign capital net inflows reached BRL 67.8 billion year-to-date, and the EU-Mercosur FTA takes effect on May 1, all presenting structural opportunities for businesses in Brazil.

事件一Event 1 高热度High

一季度巴中贸易创历史新高，石油出口翻倍 Q1 Brazil-China Trade Hits Record High, Oil Exports Double

事件概述Overview

2026年一季度巴西对华出口达239亿美元，同比增长21.7%，创历史同期新高。其中对华石油出口额72亿美元，同比激增94.6%，出口量增长122%至1650万吨，中国吸纳了巴西57%的石油出口。除石油外，牛肉对华出口额18亿美元（+33.8%），铁合金出口4.78亿美元（量近翻倍）。同期巴西自华进口179亿美元，其中电动汽车进口额12.3亿美元，较去年同期增长7.5倍。 In Q1 2026, Brazil's exports to China reached US$23.9 billion, up 21.7% YoY, a record high. Oil exports to China totaled US$7.2 billion, surging 94.6% YoY, with volume up 122% to 16.5 million tons — China absorbed 57% of Brazil's oil exports. Beyond oil, beef exports to China reached US$1.8 billion (+33.8%), and ferroalloy exports hit US$478 million (volume nearly doubled). Meanwhile, Brazil imported US$17.9 billion from China, including US$1.23 billion in EVs, a 7.5x increase YoY.

涉及方Parties Involved

巴西外贸部（MDIC）、中国海关、Petrobras、巴西矿业协会Ibram、巴西肉类加工厂协会Abrafrigo Brazil's MDIC, China Customs, Petrobras, Ibram (Brazilian Mining Association), Abrafrigo (Brazilian Meat Processors Association)

潜在影响Potential Impact

中国作为巴西第一大贸易伙伴的地位进一步强化，中东冲突加速了中国对巴西石油的战略采购。对在巴中资企业而言，双边贸易深化有利于拓展供应链合作，但电动汽车进口激增可能引发巴西本土产业保护诉求。 China's position as Brazil's top trading partner is further strengthened, with the Middle East conflict accelerating China's strategic procurement of Brazilian oil. For Chinese enterprises in Brazil, deepening bilateral trade facilitates supply chain cooperation, but the surge in EV imports may trigger domestic industry protection demands.

事件二Event 2 极高热度Very High

2027年财政框架法案——盈余目标与债务轨迹 2027 Fiscal Framework Bill — Surplus Target & Debt Trajectory

事件概述Overview

卢拉政府向国会提交2027年预算指引法案（LDO），设定基本盈余目标为GDP的0.5%（约732亿雷亚尔），浮动区间±0.25%。但因允许扣除司法判决款、国防投资等共计657亿雷亚尔，实际盈余仅约80亿雷亚尔。卢拉执政前三年财政赤字分别为2285亿、429亿、617亿雷亚尔，总债务增长2.95万亿雷亚尔，债务占GDP比例从71.7%升至79.2%，预计2026年达83.6%。2028-2030年盈余目标分别为GDP的1%、1.25%、1.5%，支出增速上限为收入增速的70%，实际增幅不超过2.5%。 The Lula government submitted the 2027 Budget Guidelines Bill (LDO) to Congress, setting a primary surplus target of 0.5% of GDP (approximately BRL 73.2 billion), with a ±0.25% floating range. However, with deductions for court-ordered payments, defense investments, etc. totaling BRL 65.7 billion, the actual surplus is only about BRL 8 billion. In Lula's first three years, fiscal deficits were BRL 228.5B, 42.9B, and 61.7B respectively, with total debt growing BRL 2.95 trillion, and debt-to-GDP rising from 71.7% to 79.2%, projected to reach 83.6% in 2026. Surplus targets for 2028-2030 are 1%, 1.25%, and 1.5% of GDP respectively, with spending growth capped at 70% of revenue growth, not exceeding 2.5%.

涉及方Parties Involved

巴西财政部、计划预算部、国会、巴西央行 Brazil's Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Planning & Budget, Congress, Central Bank of Brazil

潜在影响Potential Impact

财政框架的"名义盈余"与"实际盈余"之间的巨大差距削弱了市场对财政纪律的信心。债务占GDP比例持续攀升（预计2029年达87.8%），可能推高巴西主权风险溢价，影响外资对巴西长期投资的信心。 The huge gap between "nominal surplus" and "actual surplus" in the fiscal framework undermines market confidence in fiscal discipline. The continuously rising debt-to-GDP ratio (projected to reach 87.8% by 2029) may push up Brazil's sovereign risk premium and affect foreign investors' confidence in long-term investment.

事件三Event 3 中高热度Med-High

Brasil Soberano 150亿雷亚尔出口商信贷线启动 Brasil Soberano BRL 15B Export Credit Line Launched

事件概述Overview

巴西政府推出"Brasil Soberano"信贷计划，提供150亿雷亚尔资金，面向受美国关税（50%）及中东战争影响的出口企业。申请条件为受影响市场营收占比不低于上年总营收5%。利率方面，大企业通过BNDES直接贷款月息1.28%，小企业1.17%；设备投资类月息更低（BNDES为1.05%/0.94%）。投资类还款期最长20年（含4年宽限期），流动资金类5年（1年宽限期）。资金来自出口担保基金FGE，由总统签署的第1.345/2026临时法案设立。 The Brazilian government launched the "Brasil Soberano" credit program, providing BRL 15 billion for exporters affected by US tariffs (50%) and the Middle East conflict. Eligibility requires affected market revenue to be at least 5% of prior year total revenue. Interest rates: large enterprises via BNDES direct loans at 1.28%/month, small enterprises at 1.17%; equipment investment rates are lower (BNDES at 1.05%/0.94%). Investment loans have up to 20-year terms (4-year grace period), working capital loans 5 years (1-year grace). Funded by the Export Guarantee Fund (FGE), established by Presidential Provisional Measure No. 1.345/2026.

涉及方Parties Involved

BNDES、巴西发展工业贸易和服务部、财政部、出口担保基金FGE BNDES, Ministry of Development, Industry, Trade & Services, Ministry of Finance, Export Guarantee Fund (FGE)

潜在影响Potential Impact

该信贷线为受地缘冲突和贸易摩擦影响的出口企业提供了流动性缓冲，尤其利好农产品、矿产品出口商。中资出口导向型企业可关注申请资格，但需注意审批流程（30天内完成）及利率条件。 This credit line provides liquidity buffer for exporters affected by geopolitical conflicts and trade friction, particularly benefiting agricultural and mineral product exporters. Chinese export-oriented enterprises should evaluate eligibility, noting the 30-day approval process and interest rate conditions.

事件四Event 4 中高热度Med-High

12%石油出口税法院裁决——外资油企面临监管不确定性 12% Oil Export Tax Court Ruling — Regulatory Uncertainty for Foreign Oil Companies

事件概述Overview

巴西联邦第二地区法院驳回外资石油企业上诉，撤销一审法院暂停12%原油出口税的禁令，恢复征收。该税旨在支撑柴油补贴预算（政府已为燃油补贴支出超300亿雷亚尔）。涉事企业包括道达尔能源（Total）等跨国石油巨头，其2月产量占巴西全国20%。企业方认为该税实为财政征收且违反事前原则，政府则主张其具备遏制通胀、稳定经济的监管功能。 Brazil's Federal Regional Court of the 2nd Region dismissed foreign oil companies' appeal, overturning the lower court's injunction suspending the 12% crude oil export tax, restoring its collection. The tax aims to support diesel subsidy budgets (the government has spent over BRL 30 billion on fuel subsidies). Affected companies include TotalEnergies and other multinational oil majors, whose February production accounted for 20% of Brazil's total. Companies argue the tax is essentially a fiscal levy violating prior notice principles, while the government maintains it serves regulatory functions of curbing inflation and stabilizing the economy.

涉及方Parties Involved

联邦第二地区法院、道达尔能源等外资油企、巴西联邦政府、Petrobras Federal Regional Court of the 2nd Region, TotalEnergies and other foreign oil companies, Brazilian Federal Government, Petrobras

潜在影响Potential Impact

该裁决加剧了外资石油企业在巴西的监管不确定性。12%的出口税直接压缩利润空间，可能影响外资对巴西深海油田的后续投资意愿。同时，米纳斯吉拉斯州3-4月柴油价格已上涨17.4%，巴西进口20-30%的柴油，能源成本压力持续传导。 The ruling intensifies regulatory uncertainty for foreign oil companies in Brazil. The 12% export tax directly compresses profit margins and may affect foreign investment appetite for Brazil's deep-sea oil fields. Meanwhile, diesel prices in Minas Gerais rose 17.4% in March-April, and Brazil imports 20-30% of its diesel, with energy cost pressures continuing to transmit.

事件五Event 5 中等热度Medium

EU-Mercosur自贸协定5月1日生效 EU-Mercosur FTA Takes Effect on May 1

事件概述Overview

欧盟-南方共同市场自由贸易协定将于2026年5月1日正式生效，将取消540余种产品的关税壁垒，预计为巴西带来超10亿美元的出口增量。该协定历经20余年谈判，是全球最大的双边自贸协定之一，覆盖超7亿人口市场。 The EU-Mercosur Free Trade Agreement will officially take effect on May 1, 2026, eliminating tariff barriers on over 540 products and expected to bring Brazil over US$1 billion in additional exports. After more than 20 years of negotiations, it is one of the world's largest bilateral FTAs, covering a market of over 700 million people.

涉及方Parties Involved

欧盟委员会、南方共同市场成员国（巴西、阿根廷、乌拉圭、巴拉圭）、巴西农业部、巴西工业联合会 European Commission, Mercosur member states (Brazil, Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay), Brazil's Ministry of Agriculture, Brazilian Industrial Confederation

潜在影响Potential Impact

协定生效将显著拓宽巴西农产品（牛肉、大豆、咖啡等）对欧出口通道，同时欧洲工业品、服务业对巴西市场准入将改善。对中资企业而言，需关注欧盟产品在巴西市场的竞争加剧，以及巴西出口结构调整带来的供应链变化。 The agreement will significantly broaden Brazil's agricultural export channels to Europe (beef, soybeans, coffee, etc.), while European industrial goods and services will gain improved market access to Brazil. For Chinese enterprises, attention should be paid to intensified competition from EU products in the Brazilian market and supply chain changes from Brazil's export structure adjustment.

事件六Event 6 高热度High

IPCA通胀预期突破目标上限，降息周期面临中断 IPCA Inflation Expectations Breach Target Ceiling, Rate-Cutting Cycle at Risk

事件概述Overview

巴西金融市场将2026年IPCA通胀预期连续第五周上调至4.71%，突破央行设定的3%目标区间上限（4.5%）。3月当月IPCA为0.88%，12个月累计4.14%。当前Selic利率为14.75%，此前已启动降息周期（上月降息0.25个百分点），但受中东冲突推高能源价格影响，市场不排除暂停甚至逆转降息。市场预计2026年底Selic降至12.5%，2029年降至9.75%。巴西全国工业联合会（CNI）将2026年GDP增长预期从1.8%上调至2%。 Brazil's financial market raised 2026 IPCA inflation expectations for the fifth consecutive week to 4.71%, breaching the central bank's 3% target range ceiling (4.5%). March IPCA was 0.88%, with 12-month cumulative at 4.14%. The current Selic rate is 14.75%, with a rate-cutting cycle already initiated (0.25pp cut last month), but the Middle East conflict pushing up energy prices means markets don't rule out pausing or even reversing cuts. Markets expect Selic to fall to 12.5% by end-2026 and 9.75% by 2029. CNI raised its 2026 GDP growth forecast from 1.8% to 2%.

涉及方Parties Involved

巴西央行货币政策委员会（Copom）、巴西地理统计局（IBGE）、CNI Central Bank's Monetary Policy Committee (Copom), IBGE (Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics), CNI

潜在影响Potential Impact

通胀预期脱锚将直接影响企业融资成本和消费信心。若降息周期中断，高利率环境将持续压制制造业投资和内需增长，对依赖巴西内需市场的外资企业构成挑战。 Unanchored inflation expectations will directly affect corporate financing costs and consumer confidence. If the rate-cutting cycle is interrupted, the high-interest-rate environment will continue to suppress manufacturing investment and domestic demand growth, posing challenges for foreign enterprises relying on Brazil's domestic market.

事件七Event 7 中等热度Medium

巴西对三类中国钢材产品征收反倾销税 Brazil Imposes Anti-Dumping Duties on Three Chinese Steel Products

事件概述Overview

巴西于2026年2月下旬发布针对中国的反倾销税，覆盖三类钢材产品，由外贸委员会（Camex）于2月13日签署。3月巴西粗钢产量281万吨，环比增长11.4%；一季度钢材进口量157万吨，同比增8.3%，年度进口量预计626万吨，为2000-2019年均值的3倍。4月钢铁工业信心指数ICIA收于51.7点，接近荣枯线，其中未来6个月预期指数升至56.6点。 Brazil issued anti-dumping duties targeting China in late February 2026, covering three categories of steel products, signed by the Foreign Trade Committee (Camex) on February 13. March crude steel production was 2.81 million tons, up 11.4% MoM; Q1 steel imports were 1.57 million tons, up 8.3% YoY, with annual imports projected at 6.26 million tons — 3x the 2000-2019 average. The April Steel Industry Confidence Index (ICIA) closed at 51.7, near the boom-bust line, with the 6-month outlook index rising to 56.6.

涉及方Parties Involved

趋势二：财政整合路径收窄，"会计技巧"支撑盈余目标 Trend 2: Fiscal Consolidation Path Narrowing, "Accounting Tricks" Supporting Surplus Targets

Statutory expenditures like pensions and minimum wage account for too high a share, while surplus targets rely on extensive deductions to "meet targets," essentially deferring fiscal pressure. The 2025 deficit already triggered fiscal rule constraints, but with limited force. If the Middle East conflict continues to push up oil prices, increased oil royalties and Petrobras dividends may improve fiscal revenue short-term, but this constitutes "windfall" rather than structural improvement. Investors should factor Brazil's sovereign credit risk into long-term investment decisions.

The rapid diversification of fertilizer supply chains (Turkmenistan, Canada, Morocco replacing Middle East sources) demonstrates Brazilian agricultural supply chain resilience, but the doubling of freight rates' impact on cold-chain exports like meat is difficult to absorb short-term. Enterprises operating in Brazil should reassess logistics cost budgets and monitor the sustainability of government fuel subsidy policies.
