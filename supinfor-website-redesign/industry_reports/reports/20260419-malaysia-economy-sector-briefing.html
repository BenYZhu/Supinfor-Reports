一、本周核心发现 1. Core Findings This Week

五大关键发现 + 核心数据 Five Key Findings + Core Data

1. 能源供应危机成为压倒性议题 1. Energy Supply Crisis Dominates the Agenda

中东冲突引发的能源供应危机成为本周压倒性议题——日耗原油70万桶、日产仅35万桶，近40%进口原油经霍尔木兹海峡。4月燃油补贴飙升至70亿令吉，全年或达720亿令吉，远超预算承受能力。 The energy supply crisis triggered by the Middle East conflict dominated this week — daily oil consumption of 700,000 barrels vs. production of only 350,000 barrels, with nearly 40% of imported crude transiting the Strait of Hormuz. April fuel subsidies surged to RM7 billion, potentially reaching RM72 billion annually, far exceeding budget capacity.

2. GDP韧性犹存但增速放缓 2. GDP Resilient but Growth Slowing

IMF上调马来西亚2026年GDP增速预测至4.7%，统计局公布一季度GDP初估增长5.3%，穆迪分析预估一季度增6.6%——基本面仍具韧性，但增速已较上季6.3%放缓，战争冲击开始显现。 IMF raised Malaysia's 2026 GDP growth forecast to 4.7%. Statistics Department reported Q1 GDP preliminary growth of 5.3%, Moody's Analytics estimated Q1 at 6.6% — fundamentals remain resilient, but growth has slowed from 6.3% last quarter as war impacts begin to show.

3. INSAP发出2008年来最严厉通胀警告 3. INSAP Issues Severest Inflation Warning Since 2008

策略分析与政策研究所（INSAP）警告通胀率或飙至6.1%-6.6%，为2008年来最糟，492万户家庭将在三四季度受到冲击。建议恢复GST并建立战略石油储备。 INSAP warned inflation could surge to 6.1%-6.6%, the worst since 2008, with 4.92 million households to be impacted in Q3-Q4. Recommended restoring GST and establishing strategic petroleum reserves.

4. 各州密集推出应对措施 4. States Roll Out Crisis Response Measures

雪兰莪1.3亿令吉韧性配套、砂拉越追加1.36亿令吉SKAS援助、沙巴上调柴油补贴至每月400令吉、霹雳州投资额创157亿令吉历史新高。 Selangor launched RM130M resilience package, Sarawak added RM136M SKAS aid, Sabah raised diesel subsidy to RM400/month, Perak investment hit record RM15.7 billion.

5. 金融市场表现分化 5. Financial Markets Show Divergence

Bank Rakyat派发10年最高18%股息，MTT航运募资6.5亿令吉成物流领域13年来最大IPO，但外资上周净卖出6369万令吉，房贷申请同比大跌22.5%。 Bank Rakyat declared 10-year high 18% dividend, MTT Shipping raised RM650M in logistics sector's largest IPO in 13 years, but foreign investors net sold RM63.69M last week, mortgage applications plunged 22.5% YoY.

二、焦点事件深度解读 2. Focal Events Deep Dive

焦点一Focus 1 高影响High Impact

2.1 能源供应危机：从石油净进口国困境到财政补贴悬崖 2.1 Energy Supply Crisis: From Net Oil Importer Dilemma to Fiscal Subsidy Cliff

事件脉络 Event Context

本周能源安全报道占据了经济栏目的绝对主导地位。财政部确认马来西亚日产原油35万桶、日耗70万桶，内需为产量两倍；48%原油本地生产，38%经霍尔木兹海峡进口。中东冲突导致该航道受阻，油价上涨近40%，物流与保险成本攀升。Petronas紧急调度载有100万桶伊拉克巴士拉原油的Ocean Thunder号抵达，可产出约1.59亿升石油产品，确认全国加油站燃油供应充足至2026年6月底。 Energy security reporting dominated economic coverage this week. The Finance Ministry confirmed Malaysia produces 350,000 barrels/day but consumes 700,000 barrels/day — domestic demand is double production; 48% crude is locally produced, 38% imported via the Strait of Hormuz. The Middle East conflict has disrupted this route, pushing oil prices up nearly 40% with rising logistics and insurance costs. Petronas urgently dispatched the Ocean Thunder carrying 1 million barrels of Iraqi Basra crude, capable of producing approximately 159 million liters of petroleum products, confirming nationwide fuel supply is secure until end of June 2026.

财政压力 Fiscal Pressure

2026年预算按油价65-70美元/桶设定补贴基准，现油价超100美元，月补贴从战前7亿令吉飙升至70亿令吉。4个月已耗尽全年84亿令吉补贴预算，若持续全额补贴将超720亿令吉，远超教育和卫生部年度拨款总和。INSAP指出补贴成本增收覆盖比仅约27%，取消RON95汽油补贴将推高通胀约6.3个百分点。 The 2026 budget set subsidy benchmarks at USD 65-70/barrel, but current prices exceed USD 100. Monthly subsidies surged from pre-war RM700 million to RM7 billion. Four months have exhausted the annual RM8.4 billion subsidy budget; full subsidies would exceed RM72 billion annually, surpassing combined Education and Health Ministry allocations. INSAP noted subsidy cost-to-revenue coverage ratio is only about 27%; removing RON95 petrol subsidies would push inflation up approximately 6.3 percentage points.

政策应对 Policy Response

上调BUDI柴油援助至每月400令吉（惠及15万柴油车主），提高稻农犁地奖励每公顷300令吉（惠及近20万稻农），放宽农机柴油购买限制。同时推出公务员居家办公、调高空调温度等节能措施。央行行长表示即便冲突近期结束，油价或仍将长期高企。通过BUDI95补贴、清洁能源出口及令吉走强缓解通胀传导。 Raised BUDI diesel aid to RM400/month (benefiting 150,000 diesel vehicle owners), increased paddy farmer plowing incentive to RM300/hectare (benefiting nearly 200,000 farmers), relaxed agricultural machinery diesel purchase restrictions. Also introduced civil servant work-from-home and higher air-conditioning temperature energy-saving measures. BNM Governor stated oil prices may remain elevated long-term even if conflict ends soon. Inflation transmission mitigated through BUDI95 subsidies, clean energy exports, and ringgit strengthening.

后续走向研判 Forward Outlook

燃油供应可维持至6月底，但6月后的供应保障方案尚未明确。若油价持续在100美元以上，政府面临两难：维持补贴则财政赤字或超GDP的4%，削减补贴则通胀失控。INSAP提出的GST恢复建议（预计年收770亿令吉）和战略石油储备可行性研究，成为中期政策方向。 Fuel supply can be maintained until end of June, but post-June supply assurance plans remain unclear. If oil prices persist above USD 100, the government faces a dilemma: maintaining subsidies could push fiscal deficit beyond 4% of GDP, while cutting subsidies risks runaway inflation. INSAP's GST restoration proposal (estimated annual revenue RM77 billion) and strategic petroleum reserve feasibility study have become medium-term policy directions.

焦点二Focus 2

2.2 一季度GDP初估5.3%：韧性犹存但战争冲击开始显现 2.2 Q1 GDP Preliminary 5.3%: Resilience Remains but War Impact Emerging

事件脉络 Event Context

4月17日，马来西亚统计局发布2026年一季度GDP初估数据，同比增长5.3%，低于市场预期的5.5%和上季度的6.3%。Bloomberg报道称"战争开始在经济中显现压力"。同周，IMF将马来西亚2026年全年GDP增速预测从4.3%上调至4.7%，世行已上调至4.4%，穆迪分析预估一季度增长6.6%。 On April 17, Malaysia's Statistics Department released Q1 2026 GDP preliminary data showing 5.3% YoY growth, below market expectations of 5.5% and last quarter's 6.3%. Bloomberg reported "war is beginning to show pressure in the economy." Same week, IMF raised Malaysia's 2026 full-year GDP forecast from 4.3% to 4.7%, World Bank raised to 4.4%, Moody's Analytics estimated Q1 growth at 6.6%.

结构分析 Structural Analysis

五大主要行业增长均有所放缓——服务业增5.4%（为GDP增长锚点），制造业增5.8%（受电子电气行业AI需求驱动），建筑业放缓至7.8%，农业增2.8%，矿业转为负增长-1.1%（因油气减产）。3月CPI同比涨1.7%，交通运输类从通缩转为通胀1.6%，是推升CPI主因。358种商品涨价，8个州属通胀高于全国水平。 All five major sectors showed slowing growth — Services grew 5.4% (GDP growth anchor), Manufacturing grew 5.8% (driven by E&E sector AI demand), Construction slowed to 7.8%, Agriculture grew 2.8%, Mining turned negative at -1.1% (due to oil & gas production cuts). March CPI rose 1.7% YoY, with transportation shifting from deflation to 1.6% inflation as the main CPI driver. 358 commodities saw price increases, with 8 states recording inflation above the national level.

多方研判 Multi-Party Assessment

HSBC维持全年GDP增长4.5%预期；RHBIB预计4.7%，认为马来西亚是东盟受冲突影响最小经济体；OCBC预计4.4%。央行行长强调经济基础稳固，私人消费、投资、出口为增长主力，旅游业恢复至疫前水平。但多位经济学家警告，若油价升至140美元/桶，GDP增速或降至约4.0%。国家银行将于5月15日正式公布一季度GDP数据。 HSBC maintains full-year GDP growth at 4.5%; RHBIB forecasts 4.7%, considering Malaysia the least conflict-affected ASEAN economy; OCBC forecasts 4.4%. BNM Governor emphasized solid economic fundamentals with private consumption, investment, and exports as growth pillars, tourism recovered to pre-pandemic levels. However, multiple economists warned if oil reaches USD 140/barrel, GDP growth could drop to approximately 4.0%. BNM will officially release Q1 GDP data on May 15.

研判：短期内经济韧性仍可维持，但二三季度将是关键考验期——通胀传导效应将在三四季度全面显现，劳动力市场或在二季度承压。央行面临稳增长与控通胀的两难抉择，维持政策利率不变。 Assessment: Economic resilience can be maintained short-term, but Q2-Q3 will be the critical test — inflation transmission effects will fully materialize in Q3-Q4, labor market may face pressure in Q2. BNM faces the dilemma of balancing growth stability and inflation control, maintaining policy rate unchanged.

焦点三Focus 3 严厉警告Severe Warning

2.3 通胀风暴预警：INSAP发出2008年以来最严厉警告 2.3 Inflation Storm Warning: INSAP Issues Severest Alert Since 2008

事件脉络 Event Context

策略分析与政策研究所（INSAP）总监温京才本周发布多份分析报告，警告马来西亚正面临"史上最严重石油供应中断"。柴油零售价7周涨124.7%，预测整体通胀率将达6.1%-6.6%，为2008年来最糟。生活成本全面影响将在2026年三四季度显现，492万户家庭受冲击。 INSAP Director Wan Jingcai released multiple analysis reports this week, warning Malaysia faces "the most severe oil supply disruption in history." Diesel retail prices rose 124.7% over 7 weeks, with overall inflation forecast at 6.1%-6.6%, the worst since 2008. Full cost-of-living impact will materialize in Q3-Q4 2026, affecting 4.92 million households.

影响传导路径 Impact Transmission Path

柴油涨价→饲料化肥成本上升→食品供应链成本转嫁→消费品价格上涨。B40低收入家庭每月实际收入损失约165令吉（占中位数收入4.8%），M40中收入家庭损失242至355令吉。现有RON95补贴无法解决问题，BUDI计划也无法覆盖无柴油车却受食品涨价影响的家庭。 Diesel price increase → Feed/fertilizer cost rise → Food supply chain cost pass-through → Consumer goods price increase. B40 low-income households face monthly real income loss of approximately RM165 (4.8% of median income), M40 middle-income households lose RM242-355. Existing RON95 subsidies cannot solve the problem, and BUDI plans cannot cover families without diesel vehicles but affected by food price increases.

三、重要动态扫描 3. Key Developments Scan

3.1 宏观经济与财政 3.1 Macroeconomy & Fiscal Policy

IMF、世行双双上调增长预测 IMF & World Bank Both Raise Growth Forecasts

IMF将2026年GDP增速预测上调至4.7%（+0.4个百分点），世行上调至4.4%，央行维持4%-5%区间。马来西亚2025年GDP增长5.2%，财政赤字改善至3.7%，失业率降至10年最低2.9%，通胀率维持1.4%低位，令吉成亚洲表现最佳货币。 IMF raised 2026 GDP growth forecast to 4.7% (+0.4pp), World Bank raised to 4.4%, BNM maintains 4%-5% range. Malaysia's 2025 GDP grew 5.2%, fiscal deficit improved to 3.7%, unemployment fell to 10-year low of 2.9%, inflation maintained at 1.4% low, ringgit became Asia's best-performing currency.

GST讨论加速 GST Discussion Accelerates

INSAP明确建议启动12个月GST公众咨询，预计5%税率年收约770亿令吉，可产生结构性盈余。议题态势从学术讨论进入政策倡议阶段。 INSAP explicitly recommended launching 12-month GST public consultation, with estimated 5% rate generating ~RM77B annual revenue, creating structural surplus. The topic has shifted from academic discussion to policy advocacy stage.

补贴体系面临结构性挑战 Subsidy System Faces Structural Challenge

4月燃油补贴70亿令吉，4个月耗尽全年预算。专家指出布伦特原油均价持续在105-120美元，需调整2026年预算，财政赤字或超GDP的4%。政府仍有财政调整空间（疫情期间曾将赤字推高至6.1%-6.4%），但长期依赖补贴不可持续。 April fuel subsidy hit RM7B, exhausting annual budget in 4 months. Experts note Brent crude averaging USD 105-120 requires 2026 budget adjustment, fiscal deficit may exceed 4% of GDP. Government retains fiscal adjustment room (deficit reached 6.1%-6.4% during pandemic), but long-term subsidy dependence is unsustainable.

3.2 贸易与投资 3.2 Trade & Investment

清真产业出口创新高 Halal Industry Exports Hit Record High

2025年清真产业出口达685.2亿令吉，同比增10.9%，占全国总出口4.3%。食品饮料板块贡献超五成，棕榈油衍生品出口大增55%。前五大出口市场为中国、新加坡、美国、日本、印尼。HDC将依据2030年清真产业总体规划推进产业升级。 2025 halal industry exports reached RM68.52B, up 10.9% YoY, accounting for 4.3% of total national exports. Food & beverage sector contributed over 50%, palm oil derivatives exports surged 55%. Top 5 export markets: China, Singapore, US, Japan, Indonesia. HDC will advance industry upgrading per the 2030 Halal Industry Master Plan.

马澳能源安全合作深化 Malaysia-Australia Energy Security Cooperation Deepens

澳总理阿尔巴尼斯访马3天，双方聚焦能源安全合作。澳供应马96%进口液化天然气，马供应澳约17%燃油，2025年双边贸易额786.3亿令吉。双方将探讨能源供应链稳定、可再生能源合作及清真红肉领域合作。 Australian PM Albanese visited Malaysia for 3 days, focusing on energy security cooperation. Australia supplies 96% of Malaysia's LNG imports, Malaysia supplies ~17% of Australia's fuel. 2025 bilateral trade reached RM78.63B. Both sides will explore energy supply chain stability, renewable energy cooperation, and halal red meat collaboration.

中马经贸关系持续深化 China-Malaysia Economic Relations Continue to Deepen

中国连续16年为马最大贸易伙伴，2025年贸易总额突破3060亿令吉，但对华仍有千亿逆差。沈志勤强调厦门大学宏观经济预测报告可助马制定2030新工业蓝图，双方在数字经济、AI领域合作深化。 China has been Malaysia's largest trading partner for 16 consecutive years, with 2025 total trade exceeding RM306B, though Malaysia maintains a ~RM100B deficit. Minister Shen emphasized Xiamen University's macroeconomic forecast report can help Malaysia formulate the 2030 New Industrial Blueprint, with deepening cooperation in digital economy and AI.

出口前景承压 Export Outlook Under Pressure

美国新增关税、中东冲突推高出口成本，霍尔木兹海峡紧张推高物流成本。美国301调查针对半导体、光伏等行业，马来西亚橡胶手套、棕榈油等产业面临关税风险。工业生产指数增速放缓，仅半导体行业提供部分出口支撑。 US new tariffs and Middle East conflict push up export costs, Strait of Hormuz tensions raise logistics costs. US Section 301 investigation targets semiconductors, solar panels; Malaysia's rubber gloves, palm oil industries face tariff risks. Industrial production index growth slowing, only semiconductor sector provides partial export support.

3.3 金融市场与企业动态 3.3 Financial Markets & Corporate Developments

Bank Rakyat派发10年最高股息 Bank Rakyat Declares 10-Year High Dividend

2025财年派发18%股息，总额5.47亿令吉，惠及78万余会员。税前利润19亿令吉（+2%），总资产1310亿令吉（+6.6%），将推进LEAD30战略聚焦可持续包容性增长。 FY2025 declared 18% dividend totaling RM547M, benefiting 780,000+ members. Pre-tax profit RM1.9B (+2%), total assets RM131B (+6.6%), advancing LEAD30 strategy focusing on sustainable inclusive growth.

IPO市场活跃 IPO Market Active

MTT航运物流以每股1.3令吉上市，募资6.5亿令吉，为物流领域13年来最大IPO，市值26亿令吉，55%资金用于收购12艘新造货柜船。Empire Premium Food首日溢价57.1%，募资1.53亿令吉。今年马股IPO筹集约37亿令吉，同比增近3倍。IAQ集团拟2027年首季IPO，筹资约10亿令吉。 MTT Shipping listed at RM1.30/share, raising RM650M — logistics sector's largest IPO in 13 years, market cap RM2.6B, 55% of funds for acquiring 12 new container vessels. 四、趋势研判 4. Trend Analysis

1

能源危机正在重塑财政与补贴体系 Energy Crisis Reshaping Fiscal & Subsidy System

从石油净出口国到净进口国的身份转变（2022年起），叠加中东冲突推高油价，燃油补贴体系面临结构性挑战。4月单月补贴70亿令吉的数字已说明现行模式不可持续。INSAP的GST恢复建议、央行行长"油价将长期高企"的判断、以及多方呼吁建立战略石油储备，均指向同一方向：需要从应急式补贴转向结构性财政改革。这一转型政治敏感性极高，但经济必要性已无法回避。 The identity shift from net oil exporter to net importer (since 2022), compounded by Middle East conflict pushing oil prices higher, has created structural challenges for the fuel subsidy system. The RM7B single-month subsidy figure demonstrates the current model is unsustainable. INSAP's GST restoration proposal, BNM Governor's "oil prices will remain elevated long-term" assessment, and multi-party calls for strategic petroleum reserves all point in the same direction: a shift from emergency subsidies to structural fiscal reform is needed. This transition is politically sensitive but economically unavoidable.
