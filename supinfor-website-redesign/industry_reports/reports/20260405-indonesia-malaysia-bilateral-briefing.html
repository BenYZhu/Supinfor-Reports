核心判断Core Assessment

本期马印双边关系处于"温热上行"区间。中东危机的外溢效应——从地缘安全到能源价格再到公务员居家办公——意外地成为两国加速协调的粘合剂。安华专访雅加达和新任防长首访印尼两大高层互动，标志着双边关系正从传统的邻国管理模式向战略伙伴协调模式升级。民生栏目的高占比（45.5%）则提醒我们，劳工权益、跨境雾霾等"低政治"议题仍是双边关系的基本盘，需要持续关注和管理。 Bilateral relations are in a "warm upswing" zone this period. The spillover effects of the Middle East crisis — from geopolitical security to energy prices to civil servant work-from-home policies — have unexpectedly become the glue accelerating coordination between the two nations. The two major high-level interactions — Anwar's special visit to Jakarta and the new Armed Forces Chief's inaugural visit to Indonesia — signal that bilateral relations are upgrading from a traditional neighborly management model to a strategic partner coordination model. The high share of the Livelihood channel (45.5%) reminds us that "low politics" issues such as labor rights and cross-border haze remain the bedrock of bilateral relations, requiring sustained attention and management.

本报告周期内，马来西亚与印度尼西亚双边关系呈现"高层引领、多线并进"的活跃态势。安华首相应普拉博沃总统之邀专访雅加达，中东危机成为双边战略协调的催化剂；新任武装部队总司令马力克首访印尼，提出马印新三边防务合作升级方案；联黎维和部队遇袭事件使两国在国际安全领域的共同关切进一步凸显。与此同时，社交媒体年龄限制政策对标、SIBS 首次出海万隆、沙巴-北加里曼丹蓝色经济合作等议题，展现了双边关系从政治安全向数字治理、经贸平台、边境发展等多维度纵深拓展的趋势。民生栏目高占比反映了两国在劳工流动、跨境雾霾等结构性议题上的持续互动。 During this reporting period, Malaysia-Indonesia bilateral relations exhibited an active posture characterized by "high-level leadership driving multi-track progress." Prime Minister Anwar visited Jakarta at President Prabowo's invitation, with the Middle East crisis serving as a catalyst for bilateral strategic coordination. The new Armed Forces Chief Malik made Indonesia his inaugural overseas visit, proposing an upgrade to the Malaysia-Indonesia-Singapore trilateral defense cooperation framework. The UNIFIL peacekeeping attack further highlighted shared concerns in international security. Meanwhile, topics such as social media age restriction policy benchmarking, SIBS's first overseas edition in Bandung, and Sabah-North Kalimantan blue economy cooperation demonstrated the trend of bilateral relations expanding from political security into digital governance, trade platforms, and border development. The high share of the Livelihood channel reflects sustained interaction on structural issues such as labor mobility and cross-border haze.

安华专访雅加达——中东危机催化双边战略协调 Anwar's Special Visit to Jakarta — Middle East Crisis Catalyzes Bilateral Strategic Coordination

3月27日，马来西亚首相安华应印尼总统普拉博沃之邀专访雅加达。此次访问由普拉博沃于3月23日致电邀请，议程聚焦中东地缘政治（巴勒斯坦局势、伊朗问题）及双边合作深化，同时为马来西亚主办第14届马印年度磋商做前期准备。 On March 27, Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar visited Jakarta at the invitation of Indonesian President Prabowo. The visit was initiated by Prabowo's phone call on March 23, with the agenda focusing on Middle East geopolitics (Palestine situation, Iran issues) and deepening bilateral cooperation, while also preparing for Malaysia's hosting of the 14th Malaysia-Indonesia Annual Consultation.

关键细节Key Details

  • •普拉博沃主动致电邀请，体现印尼方面对双边协调的迫切需求Prabowo proactively called to invite, reflecting Indonesia's urgent need for bilateral coordination
  • •议题核心为中东局势，特别是巴勒斯坦和伊朗问题的东盟立场协调Core agenda focused on the Middle East situation, particularly ASEAN stance coordination on Palestine and Iran issues
  • •安华强调东盟团结与集体行动的重要性Anwar emphasized the importance of ASEAN unity and collective action
  • •双方就第14届马印年度磋商的议程框架进行了初步沟通Both sides conducted preliminary communication on the agenda framework for the 14th Malaysia-Indonesia Annual Consultation
  • •多家媒体（BERNAMA、NST、Astro Awani、东方日报）密集报道，显示事件受关注度极高Intensive coverage by multiple media outlets (BERNAMA, NST, Astro Awani, Oriental Daily), indicating extremely high attention

洞察Insight

此次专访的深层意义在于，中东危机正在重塑东南亚国家间的协调逻辑。马印两国作为东盟最大的穆斯林人口国家，在巴勒斯坦问题上有着天然的立场共鸣，而中东冲突引发的能源价格波动又直接冲击两国经济。安华与普拉博沃选择在年度磋商之前增加一次"非正式但高规格"的面对面沟通，表明双边关系正从制度化的年度磋商框架向更灵活的战略协调模式演进。这一趋势值得持续跟踪。 The deeper significance of this visit lies in how the Middle East crisis is reshaping the coordination logic among Southeast Asian nations. As ASEAN's two largest Muslim-majority countries, Malaysia and Indonesia share a natural resonance on the Palestine issue, while energy price volatility triggered by Middle East conflicts directly impacts both economies. Anwar and Prabowo's decision to add an "informal but high-level" face-to-face meeting before the annual consultation indicates that bilateral relations are evolving from an institutionalized annual consultation framework toward a more flexible strategic coordination model. This trend warrants continued tracking.

马印新三边防务合作升级——新任武装部队总司令首访印尼 Malaysia-Indonesia-Singapore Trilateral Defense Upgrade — New Armed Forces Chief's Inaugural Visit to Indonesia

4月2日，马来西亚新任武装部队总司令马力克·拉扎克·苏莱曼上将首次正式出访即选择印尼，提出加强马来西亚-印度尼西亚-新加坡三边军事合作的倡议。 On April 2, Malaysia's new Armed Forces Chief General Malik Razak Sulaiman chose Indonesia for his inaugural official overseas visit, proposing an initiative to strengthen Malaysia-Indonesia-Singapore trilateral military cooperation.

关键细节Key Details

  • •马力克就3名印尼维和人员在黎巴嫩殉职表示慰问，展现两军情谊Malik expressed condolences for the 3 Indonesian peacekeepers killed in Lebanon, demonstrating military camaraderie

新任防长首访选择印尼而非传统的"五眼"盟友，释放了明确的战略信号：马来西亚正在重新校准其防务外交的优先序，将东盟近邻置于更核心的位置。从双边"空中之眼"到三边防务框架的升级构想，反映了马六甲海峡安全治理从"各管一段"向"联合管控"的演进方向。5月边境委员会会议将是检验这一构想能否落地的关键节点。 The new defense chief's choice of Indonesia over traditional "Five Eyes" allies for his inaugural visit sends a clear strategic signal: Malaysia is recalibrating its defense diplomacy priorities, placing ASEAN neighbors in a more central position. The upgrade vision from bilateral "Eyes in the Sky" to a trilateral defense framework reflects the evolution of Malacca Strait security governance from "each managing their own section" to "joint management." The May Border Committee meeting will be a critical juncture for testing whether this vision can be implemented.

联黎维和部队遇袭——马印共同面对的安全挑战 UNIFIL Peacekeeping Force Attacked — A Shared Security Challenge for Malaysia and Indonesia

3月29日至4月5日期间，联合国驻黎巴嫩临时部队（UNIFIL）遭受袭击，3名印尼维和人员殉职、3人受伤。马来西亚驻黎维和部队 MALBATT 850-13 随即接到停止行动的命令。 Between March 29 and April 5, the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) was attacked, resulting in 3 Indonesian peacekeepers killed and 3 wounded. Malaysia's peacekeeping contingent MALBATT 850-13 in Lebanon was immediately ordered to cease operations.

关键细节Key Details

  • •3名印尼维和人员在黎巴嫩南部执行任务时殉职，另有3人受伤3 Indonesian peacekeepers were killed while on duty in southern Lebanon, with 3 others wounded
  • •马来西亚强烈谴责袭击行为，称其违反国际法Malaysia strongly condemned the attack, calling it a violation of international law
  • •MALBATT 850-13 被命令暂停行动，保障人员安全MALBATT 850-13 was ordered to suspend operations to ensure personnel safety
  • •马力克上将在访问印尼期间专门就此事表示慰问General Malik specifically expressed condolences on this matter during his visit to Indonesia
  • •事件凸显两国在联合国维和行动中的共同承诺与共同风险The incident highlights both countries' shared commitment and shared risks in UN peacekeeping operations

洞察Insight

维和部队遇袭事件虽发生在中东，却在马印双边关系中产生了独特的"共情效应"。两国同为联合国维和行动的积极贡献者，印尼维和人员的牺牲引发了马来西亚从官方到民间的广泛同情。这种在国际安全领域的"命运共同体"体验，为双边关系注入了超越经贸利益的情感纽带。马力克访印尼期间专门致哀的安排，更将这种共情转化为具体的防务外交行动。 Although the peacekeeping attack occurred in the Middle East, it produced a unique "empathy effect" in Malaysia-Indonesia bilateral relations. Both countries are active contributors to UN peacekeeping operations, and the sacrifice of Indonesian peacekeepers triggered widespread sympathy from both official and public circles in Malaysia. This "community of shared destiny" experience in international security has injected an emotional bond into bilateral relations that transcends economic interests. General Malik's specific arrangement to pay tribute during his Indonesia visit further transformed this empathy into concrete defense diplomacy action.

🟡 中影响Medium Impact 事件四Event 4

跨境雾霾再起——加里曼丹热点与砂拉越空气质量恶化 Cross-Border Haze Returns — Kalimantan Hotspots and Sarawak Air Quality Deterioration

3月28日至29日，砂拉越多地空气质量恶化，17个监测区域达到中等污染水平，主要原因为印尼加里曼丹地区检测到241个热点。 From March 28 to 29, air quality deteriorated across multiple areas in Sarawak, with 17 monitoring zones reaching moderate pollution levels, primarily due to 241 hotspots detected in Indonesia's Kalimantan region.

关键细节Key Details

  • •印尼加里曼丹检测到241个热点，砂拉越境内18个热点241 hotspots detected in Indonesia's Kalimantan, 18 hotspots within Sarawak
  • •砂拉越17个区域空气污染指数（API）达到中等水平17 areas in Sarawak reached moderate Air Pollution Index (API) levels
  • •正值季风过渡期，大气条件不利于污染物扩散During the monsoon transition period, atmospheric conditions are unfavorable for pollutant dispersion
  • •砂拉越自然资源与环境委员会（NREB）启动雾霾作战室Sarawak Natural Resources and Environment Board (NREB) activated the haze operations room
  • •砂拉越暂停发放受控焚烧许可证Sarawak suspended issuance of controlled burning permits

洞察Insight

跨境雾霾是马印双边关系中最具"季节性确定性"的摩擦点。尽管两国已建立多层次的雾霾应对机制，但每年季风过渡期（3-5月及8-10月）的热点激增仍然考验着双边环境治理的有效性。本次事件中砂拉越的快速响应（启动作战室、暂停焚烧许可）表明马方已形成较为成熟的应急机制，但根本解决仍有赖于印尼方面对加里曼丹农业焚烧的源头管控。随着季风过渡期持续至5月，雾霾风险仍需持续监测。 Cross-border haze is the most "seasonally predictable" friction point in Malaysia-Indonesia bilateral relations. Despite both countries having established multi-layered haze response mechanisms, the surge in hotspots during each monsoon transition period (March-May and August-October) continues to test the effectiveness of bilateral environmental governance. Sarawak's rapid response in this incident (activating the operations room, suspending burning permits) indicates Malaysia has developed a relatively mature emergency mechanism, but fundamental resolution still depends on Indonesia's source control of agricultural burning in Kalimantan. As the monsoon transition period continues through May, haze risks require continued monitoring.

🟡 中影响Medium Impact 事件五Event 5

社交媒体年龄限制——马来西亚借鉴印尼经验 Social Media Age Restrictions — Malaysia Draws on Indonesia's Experience

3月7日，马来西亚通讯部长法赫米·法兹尔宣布计划于2026年下半年实施16岁以下社交媒体使用限制，明确表示参考了印尼的先行经验（印尼相关法规于3月28日生效）。 On March 7, Malaysian Communications Minister Fahmi Fadzil announced plans to implement social media usage restrictions for those under 16 in the second half of 2026, explicitly stating reference to Indonesia's pioneering experience (Indonesia's relevant regulations took effect on March 28).

关键细节Key Details

  • •马来西亚计划设定16岁为社交媒体使用最低年龄Malaysia plans to set 16 as the minimum age for social media use
  • •通讯部长法赫米公开赞扬印尼的做法Communications Minister Fahmi publicly praised Indonesia's approach
  • •副部长张念群表示将学习印尼的分阶段实施方案Deputy Minister Teo Nie Ching stated they would learn from Indonesia's phased implementation plan
  • •印尼成为东盟地区数字治理的"先行者"Indonesia has become the "pioneer" of digital governance in the ASEAN region
  • •政策对标体现了两国在非传统安全领域的治理经验共享Policy benchmarking reflects governance experience sharing between the two countries in non-traditional security areas

洞察Insight

社交媒体年龄限制看似技术性议题，实则折射出马印两国在数字治理领域正在形成一种新型的"政策学习"关系。印尼凭借其2.7亿人口的市场规模率先立法，马来西亚则以"快速跟随者"姿态借鉴调整。这种模式可能在人工智能监管、数据跨境流动等更多数字治理议题上复制，形成东盟内部的"政策扩散"效应。 While social media age restrictions may appear to be a technical issue, they actually reflect a new type of "policy learning" relationship forming between Malaysia and Indonesia in the digital governance domain. Indonesia, leveraging its 270-million-strong market, took the lead in legislation, while Malaysia adopted a "fast follower" posture to learn and adapt. This pattern may be replicated across more digital governance topics such as AI regulation and cross-border data flows, creating a "policy diffusion" effect within ASEAN.

沙巴-北加里曼丹蓝色经济合作 Sabah-North Kalimantan Blue Economy Cooperation

洞察Insight

沙巴-北加里曼丹的蓝色经济合作代表了马印双边关系的一个新增长极——"边境驱动型合作"。不同于传统的首都对首都外交，这种由边境州省主导、聚焦海洋经济和碳交易的合作模式，更贴近两国共享的婆罗洲生态系统现实。Serikin 边境设施的升级将为这一合作提供硬件支撑，值得中长期跟踪。 The Sabah-North Kalimantan blue economy cooperation represents a new growth pole in Malaysia-Indonesia bilateral relations — "border-driven cooperation." Unlike traditional capital-to-capital diplomacy, this cooperation model led by border states/provinces and focused on marine economy and carbon trading is more aligned with the shared Borneo ecosystem reality. The Serikin border facility upgrade will provide hardware support for this cooperation, warranting medium-to-long-term tracking.

三、趋势观察III. Trend Watch

4 项关键趋势研判4 key trend assessments

趋势一：中东危机成为马印双边关系的"加速器" Trend 1: Middle East Crisis as an "Accelerator" for Malaysia-Indonesia Relations
