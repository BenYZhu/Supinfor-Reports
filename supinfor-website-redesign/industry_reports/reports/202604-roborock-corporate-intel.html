一、执行摘要I. Executive Summary

本周期内，石头科技呈现三个核心特征：产品矩阵从室内清洁向全场景扩张、中端市场密集布局抢占增量、全球渠道网络持续深化。品牌以Qrevo S Pro为代表的中端新品在欧洲多国同步上市，割草机器人RockMow/RockNeo系列正式进入波兰、德国等市场，标志着石头科技从"扫地机器人品牌"向"全场景智能清洁平台"的战略升级。During this period, Roborock exhibited three core characteristics: product matrix expanding from indoor cleaning to full-scene coverage, intensive mid-range market deployment to capture growth, and continued deepening of global channel networks. The mid-range Qrevo S Pro launched simultaneously across multiple European countries, while the RockMow/RockNeo robotic mower series officially entered Poland, Germany and other markets, marking Roborock's strategic upgrade from a "robot vacuum brand" to a "full-scene smart cleaning platform."

机会判断Opportunity Assessment

石头科技的合作网络呈现"品牌+渠道+生态"三层结构。皇马合作提升品牌调性，Allo/燦坤拓展线下触点，Home Assistant/Matter打通智能家居生态。这种组合使Roborock不仅是一个"产品"，而是用户智能家居系统的一部分。Roborock's partnership network presents a three-layer structure of "brand + channel + ecosystem." 当前合作网络中缺少大型零售商的深度绑定（如与MediaMarkt、Best Buy的战略合作），主要依赖电商渠道（亚马逊）和品牌官网。随着产品线扩展到割草机器人等需要线下体验的品类，渠道短板可能成为增长瓶颈。The current partnership network lacks deep ties with major retailers (e.g., strategic partnerships with MediaMarkt, Best Buy), relying mainly on e-commerce channels (Amazon) and the brand website. As the product line expands to categories like robotic mowers that require offline experience, channel gaps may become growth bottlenecks.
