二、欧洲电网投资大扩张II. European Grid Investment Expansion

洞察 2.1Insight 2.1

Grid saturation has surpassed capacity shortage as Europe's top energy transition bottleneck.

六、贸易壁垒与监管VI. Trade Barriers & Regulation

洞察 6.1Insight 6.1

关键洞察：美国从关税（加征10%-15%）、反倾销（正极材料最高102.72%）、税收抵免限制（FEOC规则）、项目暂停（海上风电）四个维度同步施压中资清洁能源企业。Key Insight: The US is simultaneously pressuring Chinese clean energy companies across four dimensions: tariffs (10-15%), anti-dumping (cathode materials up to 102.72%), tax credit restrictions (FEOC rules), and project suspensions (offshore wind).

  • 美国商务部裁定中国产正极材料存在倾销，征收最高102.72%反倾销税US Commerce Dept. ruled Chinese cathode materials dumped, imposing up to 102.72% anti-dumping duties
  • Trina Solar、JA Solar已出售美国产能，Canadian Solar调整股权结构规避限制Trina Solar, JA Solar sold US capacity; Canadian Solar restructured equity to circumvent restrictions
  • 美国政府暂停多个海上风电项目，包括Iberdrola的Vineyard Wind 1US government suspended multiple offshore wind projects including Iberdrola's Vineyard Wind 1
  • 取消75亿美元可再生能源项目补贴，引发司法诉讼Cancelled $7.5B renewable energy subsidies, triggering lawsuits

欧盟首次以外国补贴规则调查中资风电企业EU Launches First FSR Investigation into Chinese Wind Company

关键洞察：欧盟对金风科技启动外国补贴规则（FSR）调查，这是首次以此规则调查中资风电企业。此前隆基绿能、上海电气、中车四方等已因FSR调查退出欧洲项目，FSR正在成为欧盟限制中资企业的系统性工具。Key Insight: The EU launched an FSR investigation into Goldwind — the first such probe into a Chinese wind company. Previously, LONGi, Shanghai Electric, and CRRC Sifang withdrew from European projects due to FSR investigations. FSR is becoming a systematic tool for the EU to restrict Chinese companies.

Chinese storage companies leverage $73/kWh cost advantage in emerging markets like Brazil, but face anti-dumping duties and FSR investigations in US/EU markets.
